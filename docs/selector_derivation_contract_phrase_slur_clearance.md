ClassDerivationBasis: physical intersection of independently drawn notation contours under the renderer's automatic phrase-slur placement
CausalFeature: an established phrase relation has unambiguous ends and an identifiable set of printed curve segments
CausalFeature: on its automatic side at least one segment overlaps the ink of neighboring notation such as a head, stem, beam or ledger line
NonCausalContextExcluded: score identity, file name, measure label, absolute page or system, particular pitches or rhythms, the number or distribution of known cases

The curve belongs to an existing phrase relation. Its contour and the neighboring ink define the defect across every printed segment, including segments separated by line or page breaks. Staff and bar lines have normal crossings. They are guide geometry.

A placement decision requires separate evidence that the opposite side clears all segments and protects neighboring ink. The decision belongs to the existing phrase writer. Synthetic evidence should include ambiguous mapping, different pages, neighbor movement, independent duplication and a shared layout conflict.

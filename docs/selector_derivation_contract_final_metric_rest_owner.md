The shared metric owner determines the run. The post-projection path admits
qualifying courses on either staff through the existing E050 writer and
receipt. Existing lower-staff selection uses the same coverage test.
Uncovered time separates runs. Established groups keep their exact claimed
notes. An independent group in the same beat may qualify on its own.

Synthetic cases cover two staves, a tied endpoint next to an isolated
attack, complete and interrupted rest coverage, exact-note conflicts,
and varied note types and tick resolutions. Corpus acceptance compares
freely released MusicXML and rendered PDF pages against r1899.

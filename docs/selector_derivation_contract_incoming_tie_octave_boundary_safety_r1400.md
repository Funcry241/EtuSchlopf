## Accepted parent and productive owner

Accepted parent is r1399, take SHA-256
988d2ab814314356ca3e744b47f69f0511c168379f06edf96b404d260740a350.
The cited error-class definition is unchanged. This is an existing planner safety
restriction, not a new classifier, writer, receipt issuer or source identity.
The pre-K00 model already owns Event.tie_stop. Register observation carries that
boolean without reconstructing any tie predecessor from pitch or XML position.
After all existing semantic and sounding-island plans have been generated,
`src/registernotation/mod.rs` refuses every complete plan with a protected endpoint.
Unmatched incoming stops are protected too; absence of a matched predecessor does
not authorize changing the display mapping at their onset. Interior continuations
under a constant span remain allowed. Retained island counts describe retained plans.

Only part/staff scope and canonical time compare the already authored boundary
with the already authored continuation. They do not mint an occurrence, parent,
role or repair identity. Hidden events, other technical voices, chord members and
the following measure are included. No new pitch/voice matching is introduced.

The existing sight projection and BLOCK #50 materialization apply only the retained
original plans. Their current journals, rhythmic representation transports, receipts,
fatal SSC checks and adoption remain unchanged. A refused complete plan has no write
and mints no receipt. The actual-owner synthetic test also proves that an ordinary
admitted transformation still requires its real original receipt coverage.

## Bounds and proof

No span is moved, shortened, extended or replaced; no pitch, staff, technical voice,
rest, cursor or tie element is edited. No force fallback, artificial chord host,
canonical split or weakening of a capacity/staff/material readback is introduced.
The independent capacity and chord-scoped staff-projection defects remain open.
This filter closes a planner safety hole, not the full unforced PDF contract.

Normative tests are synthetic. They cover renamed labels, scaled units, a hidden
chord continuation in the following measure, independent parts/staves and their
duplication, a permitted internal continuation, time-relocated endpoints, and the
actual unchanged BLOCK #50 owner with real receipts. External real-score evaluation
is delivered separately and cannot authorize selector narrowing or release.

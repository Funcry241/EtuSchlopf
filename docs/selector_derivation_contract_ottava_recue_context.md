## Meaning and necessity

This is an omitted comparison context, not a finding that an existing cue is
wrong or redundant. A continued line across silence can still need a renewed
reader reminder. A physically continuous line in one PDF alone does not prove
that removing its reminder is musically preferable or safe.

Equal-display persistent scope distinguishes this question from choosing an
octave register or merging spans. The gap explains why a continuation exists.
The inherited binary bridge/restart policy explains why a reader-oriented
comparison is absent. Without any of these relations another class or no
omission may apply. No fixed duration, pitch, note count or local witness
pattern is required to define the missing decision.

The prior r605 policy remains binding in this take. Its in-system reminders
were intentional and were qualified in the corpus. Calling the new observation
a duplicate-writer bug, silently weakening its export guard or deleting words
without an independently accepted replacement decision would be incorrect.

## Positive neighbors and UNKNOWN

- A continuation after silence can require exactly the existing reminder.
- A supported final layout restart may already provide a renderer reminder;
  the installed final-layout projection remains a separate existing rule.
- A later continuation in that same measure is not a layout-restart cue merely
  because the measure begins a system.
- A change of octave display, overlapping line identities, intervening pitched
  activity or changed staff-domain ownership is not the fixed-scope comparison
  defined here. A cue-only repair cannot silently join or move those scopes.
- Missing exact planner input, gap ownership, line identity, final layout or
  visible-line evidence is UNKNOWN and cannot authorize cue suppression.
- An upper-staff gap may contain notes in the lower staff. It is not scorewide
  silence and cannot be established by summing serialized note durations.
- Independent candidates must retain their own decisions when unrelated parts
  or passages are duplicated; no global first-match or uniqueness permission.

## Later repair obligations

Resolve the responsible visible-state decision and its real productive callers
before changing code. Distinguish exact early register input, semantic plan,
visible continuation plan, final layout, writer result and persisted PDF.
Final XML cannot retroactively establish an earlier planner's evidence.

A separately accepted repair scope must justify every safety restriction and
the musical preference between the baseline reminder and any alternative. It
must not turn this definition into a blanket remove-all-continue-words rule or
invent a duration threshold from the known witness. Renderer readback observes
actual output; it does not create missing source or musical authority.

Keep note/rest bytes, pitches, durations, attacks, beams, ties, slurs, clefs,
octave start/continue/stop topology, placement, staff and line identities fixed
for a cue-only proposal. Use the existing owner and receipt/adoption paths;
introduce no PDF fix or later cleanup writer. Reconcile early materialization,
final layout projection and persisted export validation rather than bypassing
one guard. Unproved alternatives retain the accepted baseline.

Synthetic evidence must cover all supported octave displays, upper/lower domains,
short and long gaps without witness-derived cutoffs, changed/intervening scopes,
layout restarts and later same-measure entries, malformed/unknown relations,
unchanged baseline reminders, actual materialization, receipt/adoption,
committed readback and reentry. Include label renaming, relocation, irrelevant
perturbation and independent duplication. Corpus addresses remain evaluation
only. No distribution or successful repair is claimed by this definition.

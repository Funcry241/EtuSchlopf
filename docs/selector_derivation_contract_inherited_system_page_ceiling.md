## Necessity and scope

The class concerns a search restriction, not proof that any excluded layout will render. A retained page can be warranted by real geometry, readability, source safety or renderer rejection; such cases do not by themselves prove this class. The preceding plan is evidence of one realized layout, not an independently proved universal capacity.

A later repair may submit a page-only trial outside the unchanged upstream page-count frontier and inherited system-count ceiling. It must preserve every ordered system and measure membership, use current source/consumer-bound geometry and a separately justified minimum ink clearance, confirm all planned boundaries, inspect top/bottom page bounds and all adjacent system gaps, and preserve musical bytes outside an exact layout whitelist. It may not change note scale, system membership, notation, source identity or physical margins. Missing or ambiguous evidence supplies no adoption permission; the last confirmed plan remains available.

Independent documents share no search state or ranking. Unknown geometry remains unknown. No scalar k, renderer-style assumption, new implementation or geometric policy is installed here. Take B is not authorized until the minimum-clearance proposal with comparison images has been reviewed by the user. Existing accepted contracts remain unchanged; this definition does not rewrite them retroactively.

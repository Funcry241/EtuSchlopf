initial arrangement boundary. Original Rule P decisions are transported from
r1350; no new primary lifetime or branch is issued here. Every original physical
occurrence retains its course and licensed root support through restification,
staff action and metric splitting. Generated rests are continued obligations,
not imported rests classified by staff or role. Canonical pitched roles retain
their separate authority.

union is empty both before and after normalization. The ledger therefore
conserves existing obligations without invoking its legacy visible-channel gap
writer. Hidden sounding members are positive represented material. A missing
interval is an invariant failure here, not permission to infer an extra rest.
No new gap-rest creator or rest-elision permit is introduced.

The chord-cohort realization prerequisites and tests are specified separately
in selector_derivation_contract_initial_chord_course_consumer_safety_r1353.md.

canonical role/run consumers. Imported Unknown material remains unadopted;
the snapshot cannot be rebound to a later state by labels or geometry.

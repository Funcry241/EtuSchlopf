# Presentation role and serialization channel: independent identities

Contract: role_occurrence_binding_v1
Status: definition foundation; productive adoption requires a later accepted Take.
ChangeClass: KodexOnly

## Scope and existing authority

A serialization channel is a bounded consumer resource. A presentation role is
an independently authorized property of represented material. Numeric voice
labels do not establish musical voice, hand, role or omission authority.

This preservation contract supplements, and does not narrow or replace,
`docs/selector_derivation_contract_partwide_renderer_channel_capacity.md`.
That accepted causal class remains the prerequisite for a later capacity repair.
No new error classifier, repair selector, role inference or writer is introduced.
Observed corpus counts cannot authorize channel reuse. Independent role binding
does not prove that a bounded assignment exists.

## Required binding

Before allocating export channels, the existing structural owner must freeze an
exact, complete relation between each role-sensitive represented occurrence and
its already authorized role. Pitched sources alone are insufficient: notation
fragments, split/tied continuations, rests, hidden carriers and physical chord
hosts must retain their distinct obligations and provenance. A shared source or
attack may have several occurrences; these must not collapse into one binding.

The key must come from canonical lineage and exact written-occurrence identity.
It must distinguish occurrences even when onset, duration, pitch, staff or source
are equal. Values of opaque IDs may establish identity, never selection or role.
An offset into mutable XML is not a durable substitute. Every later materializing
transition must transport or positively rebind that identity to its output.

Missing, ambiguous, conflicting, stale or multiply matched provenance is Unknown
and fails closed for the affected mutation. A rest is not assigned a role by
pitch absence, its voice number, a nearby majority, or an inferred primary lane.
Existing role authority remains unchanged; this contract does not promote
Secondary to Primary or reinterpret technical temporal lanes as musical voices.

## Role-sensitive consumers

Every productive role-sensitive consumer must resolve the role from the exact
bound occurrence rather than from an export label. In particular this applies
to primary-rest exemptions, shadow-rest removal, rest readability, primary beam
partition decisions and lower primary beam continuity. Enumeration of these
examples is not permission to leave another role-sensitive path unresolved.

A rest-only or hidden pitched carrier retains its structural chord-host and
cursor obligations. The new binding cannot authorize its removal. Future channel
sharing must not change any role-sensitive decision for the same material.
Safe migration therefore requires an inventory of all label/channel-to-role
reads, including indirect predicates, plus tests at their actual writer boundary.

## Channel assignment

Different roles alone do not prove a musical or written-time conflict. After
role consumers are independent, a technical channel may carry distinct roles
at different occurrences only when complete, partwide compatibility is proved.
Conversely, matching roles do not establish compatibility.

Preserve independently authorized HARD SAME, sounding-interval separation,
written-interval separation, actual pitched chord-host bounds, tie continuity,
visible rest obligations, source/fragment/attack continuity and cursor semantics.
Test a proposed reuse against every channel member, not one representative.
Include non-pitched carriers in the complete consumer projection. No musical
constraint may be removed merely to fit a slot budget.

Consumer capacity must be derived from the established consumer/version profile
over the complete shared part/resource group. Staff-local maxima, maximum voice
number or a nominal total slot count are insufficient: ordinary and overlapping
cross-staff reservations and participating head/rest counts affect allocation.
Consumer preferences do not establish canonical staff or role authority.

An infeasible or incomplete assignment must fail closed with its obstruction or
Unknown reason; it must not omit notes/rests, merge musical identities, move hands,
fabricate hosts or enable force conversion. A bounded solver is not authorized
by this definition Take. Its implementation and deterministic, identity-neutral
tie handling need their own admitted change.

## Acceptance obligations for later implementation

Synthetic tests must cover compatible sharing with different retained roles;
same-role incompatibility; conflicting/missing occurrence bindings; distinct
fragments of one source; rest/carrier/chord-host obligations; universal reuse;
whole-part cross-staff reservations; and provable infeasibility.

Project/measure renaming, opaque identity bijections, valid voice-label renaming,
staff permutation with corresponding role/profile transport, uniform time-unit
scaling and independent-candidate duplication must preserve decisions. Duplicating
an observation is distinct from adding another musical occurrence/resource demand.
Independent resource groups cannot change each other's feasibility or permits.

The definition examples are binding-model proofs, not productive writer tests.
Later tests must traverse actual role consumers and authorized materialization,
including negative cases where sharing would alter pauses, beams or chord hosts.
Corpus data is evaluation only and must not determine features or selectors.

Final persisted master/render bytes require exact represented-occurrence and
rest/host/cursor coverage, followed by ordinary unforced consumer conversion and
native readback. Compare complete pitch spelling, onset, duration, staff, ties
and required notation/rest obligations through exact origin mappings. A lossy
normalized tuple comparison, occurrence count, PDF, schema pass or exit zero
alone is insufficient. Consumer normalization must be positively explained,
not silently equated to preservation. Retention does not establish playability.

## Foundation boundary

This Take changes documentation and Take metadata only. All accepted Rust,
canonical contracts, gates, owner capabilities, labels, role derivation,
materialization, renderer invocation and musical output remain unchanged.
The next productive Take must consume the accepted capacity class unchanged and
include a complete selector derivation contract if it changes selection.

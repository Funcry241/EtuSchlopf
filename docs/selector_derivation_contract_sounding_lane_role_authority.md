The class follows a dependency from acoustic occupancy to notation authority.
The direct sounding-overlap conflict and this transitive role dependency are
separate observable paths. A classifier must establish the actual dependency
and its consumer effect. A role name or a technical lane alone supplies
insufficient evidence of class membership.

An independently authorized semantic role may have valid sounding requirements.
Its complete origin must explain those requirements. The classifier records
that origin and distinguishes it from an unproved promotion of an allocation
artifact. Written intervals alone do not uniquely identify musical voices.

The counterfactual holds written occurrences and independent notation obligations
fixed while varying the acoustic occupancy used by the temporal lane allocator.
The relevant observation is a change of role-sensitive eligibility without a
corresponding change in independent notation evidence. Coordinates and opaque
identities carry provenance throughout that comparison.

This definition supplements the accepted sounding-only channel-conflict class
and role_occurrence_binding_v1. Existing source-role bindings retain their
current runtime interpretation until a later admitted implementation establishes
the required replacement evidence at every affected consumer.

This Stage A freezes the class before its implementation. Its distribution has
not been measured under this definition. Earlier allocation and beam-edge counts
remain evaluation observations with their original scope.

CausalFeature: the bound tonal context and actual pitched occurrence course establish an unambiguous contiguous stepwise figure with one chromatic attack between two diatonic attacks
CausalFeature: the enclosing diatonic spellings are each supported by the active signature, their letter positions are two steps apart, and both sounding intervals move by one or two semitones in the same direction

## Scope

Use the actual bound written voice and exact attack, release and onset order.
The three notes must supply distinct attacks with contiguous support, with no
rest, chord or ambiguous competing continuation in the voice. Another voice
may proceed independently. Either direction qualifies. The middle attack
must be outside the active signature's diatonic pitch set. The surrounding
notes establish two directed letter steps in their actual registers.
When one ordinary single accidental yields the original sounding pitch
on the intervening letter, the spelling follows those two letter steps.

A semitone interpolation between adjacent letter positions has no vacant
middle letter under this definition. A figure with a leap, a key boundary,
or a missing or unbound flank gives no spelling decision. If a reference
voice cannot be recovered in the actual notation course, its reference
sequence remains comparison evidence only. An explicit local obligation
or an unresolved tie-chain conflict preserves the current spelling.

## Authority and verification

The existing tonal spelling owner remains responsible for a later
implementation. A spelling change must preserve the complete original
occurrence, sounding pitch, support, staff, voice, visibility, and tie
edges. The key declaration stays exact. An unbound tied occurrence
cannot be repaired in isolation. Accidentals must be consistent with the
new notation, and one sounding key must retain one spelling inside any
affected chord. Verify the produced Render source and renderer output.

Use independent synthetic ascent and descent figures, a genuine
interpolation between adjacent letters, and a missing or displaced
flank. Relocation and independent duplication must preserve local
decisions. A later selector-sensitive take must reference this unchanged
definition in its accepted parent.

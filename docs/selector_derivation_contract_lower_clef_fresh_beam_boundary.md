## Parent and meaning

The error-class definition was frozen in r1513 and remains byte-unchanged in the
accepted r1514 parent. Its broad missing-context class is not narrowed to known
examples. This repair supplies a conservative supported scope, not a general
permission for clef switches anywhere inside beams.

One-measure cursor closure is a safety bound: the observer has no cross-measure
duration/carry proof for new interior boundaries. Backup/forward and non-pure
attribute blocks inside the actual beam envelope, and lower octave start/stop
boundaries there, make that group unsupported. Unknown duration/staff or changing
duration units after notes makes the measure's new timing evidence unavailable.
These limits do not reclassify the parent error class or rank corpus witnesses.

The group must consist of visible pitched lower heads and their resolved lower
chord members in one serialized voice; hidden/rest/foreign-staff/foreign-voice
carriers or unsupported member beam tags leave that group unproved. The voice
text only relates the current serialized beam observations; it does not mint
Source/Attack occurrence identity or reconstruct a withdrawn canonical group.
Cross-staff mutation and reconnection are not authorized. All members must retain an originally resolved anchor. A chord member is never
an independent boundary, even if its pitch prefers another clef.

The existing tie-open guard still independently blocks inserts or retained
surfaces inside open lower ties. A beam beginning on a matched continuation can
retain its earlier tie origin; only a later proved fresh head may expose its own
reading boundary. This does not move a clef onto the continuation or alter a tie. Outgoing ties on
the newly exposed head are also outside this repair scope: admitting them would
change stored tie-origin transport. Untied-only admission keeps both original
tie and primary-beam origin maps unchanged; only effective clef boundaries can
differ. Existing owner pressure values, including the actual octave mapping,
establish mixed preferences; no new pitch or ledger threshold is introduced.

## Complete comparison and materialization

The original scanner policy with an empty boundary set and the original solver
guards computes the baseline. Extended candidate formation additionally unions
the original candidate choices, so the prior feasible course is not silently
removed by splitting anchor observations. The original costs and tie-breaks stay
unchanged. Equal or worse total cost preserves the baseline, and unavailable
baseline/extended evidence cannot grant a new course.

The existing E024 and FinalNotation materializers retain their write roots,
note/clef invariants, source matching, journals and receipt/adoption paths.
Pure clef surfaces immediately before a proved head are recognized from the
current document, not from a trusted marker. Timing, group and chord relations
are recomputed on reentry. The old physical primary-beam observer remains an
observer; its Outside result by itself grants no new interior candidate.
Unknown scope handling for existing baseline surfaces remains unchanged.

Pitch, spelling, onset, duration, visibility, staff/voice, rest, beam levels and
membership, ties, slurs and octave directions acquire no write permission. No new
writer, PDF fix, phase feedback, source-identity reconstruction or #57 relocation
operator is introduced. This take does not weaken build gates or alter their pins.

## Evidence obligations

Independent synthetic cases cover both clef directions, tie-to-beam continuation,
incoming ties on heads and members, chords, hidden/cross-staff/foreign-voice data,
cursor and octave fences, incomplete beams, conflicting temporal occupancy or another beam span,
equal/worse courses, pure versus mixed attributes, receipt-bound materialization,
reentry, label relocation and independent part duplication. Real captured inputs
are evaluation only and never normative fixtures or productive selectors.
The actual FinalNotation/receipt/adoption seam must be exercised independently
before delivery; native full-corpus and changed-page review remain required.

## Classification discipline

r1033 implements the causal class frozen in r1032 without modifying that parent contract. Exact source/attack identifiers may participate only in already-existing evidence binding; their values have no classification authority.

The classifier may measure structural precursors of the frozen class. It may not infer renderer-visible beam loss from persisted MusicXML beam tags, may not infer exact final occurrence identity from onset/duration similarity, and may not treat an existing beam encoding witness as proof of its historical canonical owner.

Accordingly, r1033 is allowed to discover a broad distribution of structural cross-voice observations while still reporting zero proven members of the full frozen class. That outcome is evidence about the missing proof boundary, not a reason to narrow the class toward a known witness.

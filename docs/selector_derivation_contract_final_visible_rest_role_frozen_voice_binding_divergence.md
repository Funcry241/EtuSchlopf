## Causal definition

This class exists when an already-visible rest is classified by the productive FinalVisibleRestRole path before the final serialized voice relation relevant to that classification is materially fixed, and the later committed-state predicate evaluates the same surviving rest under a different, already-authoritative bound voice relation.

The defect is not that the final voice plan changes. A later serialized voice binding is permitted. The defect is that the productive role decision and the release readback can disagree solely because they do not consume the same authoritative voice relation at the point where the rest-role decision becomes final.

Membership requires all of the following:

1. a concrete visible positive-duration rest survives into the productive role-decision region;
2. the productive role owner evaluates or preserves that rest without the final bound voice relation that the committed readback later uses;
3. an already-authorized final serialized voice materialization changes the relevant channel relation without changing the pitched semantic timeline;
4. under the committed FrozenSerializedVoicePlan relation the surviving rest is a positive canonical shadow-bookkeeping candidate although it was not eliminated by the earlier role owner.

Raw XML voice numbers, same-staff overlap, parseability, cursor conservation, sounding stability, candidate uniqueness, or a particular corpus coordinate are insufficient by themselves.

## Necessity review

1. The visible rest is necessary because the class concerns contradictory role truth for one surviving carrier, not creation of a new rest.
2. The pre-binding productive decision is necessary because a defect wholly inside the committed detector would be a different class.
3. The later authoritative serialized voice materialization is necessary because ordinary unrelated XML edits do not establish this ordering mismatch.
4. Pitched semantic stability is necessary to distinguish representation/role rebinding from an actual musical-content mutation.
5. A positive committed canonical shadow classification is necessary because a mere label change without role contradiction does not require repair.

## Evidence requirements for later repair scope

A later repair-scope take must reference this contract unchanged from an accepted parent. It may change only an existing owner/order/binding boundary needed to make productive role classification and committed readback consume one authoritative final serialized voice relation.

The repair must not weaken the committed `CommittedShadowRestCandidates == 0` veto, invent a parallel overlap heuristic, special-case a corpus location, or globally purge secondary-voice rests. It must preserve pitch, onset, duration, staff, final serialized voice, chord/tie semantics and cursor duration except for an already-authorized canonical shadow-rest representation change.

Normative tests derived later must be synthetic and relocatable. Independent duplication, different numeric voice labels, proportional timing, different staff/pitch values and irrelevant measure relocation must not change the local causal decision.

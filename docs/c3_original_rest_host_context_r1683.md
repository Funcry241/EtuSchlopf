# r1683 - OriginalRestHostC3Context

Parent: accepted r1682, version 0.10.1839.
ChangeClass: MusicMutation (representation choices may change).
Native acceptance: pending.

## Cause and owner

A physical MusicXML chord can inherit its onset from a non-pitched rest host.
The original beam observer deliberately records pitched chord heads only; it
does not claim a rest is an independent beam head. The C3 input nevertheless
treated every member without a pitched physical head as unresolved. A valid
rest-backed cohort consequently prevented a complete comparison for its staff.

The correction belongs to the original physical attachment observation and
its carried joint-reading input. No upstream sounding note is restored,
removed or invented. The existing actual beam/rest emitter now records a
separate rest-host range through its own original operation maps. Existing
continuations carry and validate that range. The original joint owner consumes
it without widening positive beam evidence.

## Boundary of the change

The accepted causal class and C3 policy stay byte-identical. Newly supplied
positive support can make a previously UNKNOWN comparison available. Unknown
or conflicting provenance still retains the incumbent. Cross-staff rest-host
support remains unsupported. Existing materializers and all final-source
checks remain mandatory; no new write root or later repair pass is added.

## Evaluation context

The current r1682 reports adopt lower C3 courses in two of ten projects.
Eight other lower domains report missing pitched physical heads. In the
earliest captured clef inputs, their corresponding syntactic members have
non-pitched rest hosts; this is corroborating observation in a different
epoch, not a substitute for original runtime evidence. These addresses and
counts are not productive selectors or normative fixtures.

## Validation boundary

Synthetic tests exercise original emitter evidence, preservation of positive
pitched-head semantics, host-content and operation-map refusals, carried
joint-reader binding, missing evidence and cross-staff retention. Native
full-corpus acceptance and fresh final XML/PDF comparison remain required.
No claim that every lower domain will become decidable or every PDF will
improve follows from the local tests.

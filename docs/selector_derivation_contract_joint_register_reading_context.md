## Meaning

This is a missing combined comparison, not proof that the incumbent is wrong,
that a particular alternative is feasible, or that fewer transitions always win.
An independently completed joint comparison that deliberately retains the
incumbent is a negative neighbor, even if the resulting page looks busy.

The class follows from the shared written-position mapping, not from a gesture
assumed from adjacent notes, a specific beam, or a known sequence of switches.
A rhythmic-gesture relation may add an independent obligation, but must come
from its existing musical owner in the same phase. Final PDF beams cannot
create that early relation. This definition neither changes nor replaces the
accepted register-gesture, common-span or lower-clef-boundary contracts.

A complete incumbent includes states that remain loco, existing useful octave
coverage, low and high margins, tied continuations, chord members, hidden
carriers, cross-staff dependencies and the actual following carry. A cropped
interval or optimistic independently chosen clef for each pitch cannot stand
in for the combined course. Unsupported dependencies remain UNKNOWN.

Class membership cannot be inferred from sign counts, ledger counts, final XML
appearance or a successful external re-engraving. Missing source identity or
unmatched phase evidence prevents a positive classification. The source call
structure motivates investigation; it is not a census of runtime occurrences.

## Reader preference and objective remain separate from membership

The user has preferred a stable reading reference that accepts additional
manageable ledger lines over several short clef and octave changes. That is a
reader preference to be made explicit in a later comparison policy, not a new
causal feature, an existing scalar-cost proof or authority to retune weights.

Report at least maximum ledger burden, total burden with its denominator,
clef-state changes, true octave-mapping changes, and visual reminders separately.
Repeated cues without a state change are not transitions. A single clef is not
automatically better; a real low/high excursion may require several changes.
No maximum of three ledger lines is installed from the exploratory result.

## Obligations before any productive implementation

1. Bind actual incumbent inputs at both existing decision owners. Carry known
   source and attack identities forward; final output cannot reconstruct them.
   Establish whether their epochs and domain ownership permit one comparison.
2. Define an independently closed comparison scope with explicit UNKNOWN and
   negative outcomes. A four-measure window is an illustration, not a scope rule.
3. State and justify a joint reader objective and admissible tradeoffs separately
   from this class. Do not assert that the old separate costs already prefer a
   user-approved engraving. Preserve the complete unchanged incumbent.
4. Use synthetic positive and negative examples: genuine register excursions,
   necessary double octaves, equal or worse stable courses, excessive ledger
   burden, chords and simultaneous voices, rests, hidden carriers, incoming and
   outgoing ties, unresolved cross-staff relations, silence and following carry.
   Different spellings require actual diatonic positions, not MIDI-only ledgers.
5. Probe renaming, time/measure relocation and independent duplication. Each
   local decision must remain independent of unrelated candidates. Never use
   a global winner, first-only choice, rarity test or corpus cardinality.
6. Only after freezing this definition may a governed classifier measure its
   distribution. The corpus can falsify it; it cannot refine it toward a witness.
7. Before writing, reconcile the existing RegisterNotationPlan and
   ClefReadabilityPrimaryCourse ownership, unchanged musical invariants,
   materializers, receipts, adoption, last write and final PDF readback. No new
   after-the-fact XML cleaner, PDF patch or unbound writer is authorized.

Sounding pitches, spelling, onsets, durations, visibility, staff/voice, rests,
beams, ties and slurs gain no mutation permission. A joint representation choice
must preserve the actual music and all unrelated obligations at every owner.

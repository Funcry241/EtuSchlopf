# r1551: structured input-reader admission

Parent r1549. KodexOnly; musical source and policy are unchanged.
Admission recognizes exact input operation identifiers, resolves known helpers
through the existing call graph, and keeps unresolved open-named calls closed.

The old coarse v1 proof-reader signal remains in revision snapshots, and the
anti-evasion comparison is byte-unchanged. No history record is deleted or
reinterpreted, no path/function exception is added, and no MusicMutation gains
permission to consume reports. Native acceptance remains required.

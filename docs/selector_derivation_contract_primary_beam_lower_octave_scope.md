## Independent cause and necessary facts

The accepted clef owner already states that every observed lower octave-display
start/stop is a hard clef-decision-scope boundary. The pitch/anchor scanner clears
its tie and primary-beam maps on each such event. A protection consumer of the same
clef domain cannot carry an older beam relation into that new scope merely because
its serialized voice label still matches. The class follows from this existing
scope invariant, not from a new temporal-gap or engraving preference.

All three causal facts are necessary. Without a relevant lower boundary, ordinary
same-scope beam handling remains outside this class. Without an old-scope relation,
there is no carried beam state. Without its consumption after the boundary, a stored
but unused record does not establish this planning defect. A fresh valid group in
the new scope is not made defective by the presence of an earlier boundary.

The scope of the queried position matters. A future boundary must not retroactively
release independently established protection at an earlier position in the old
scope. Handling of an unclosed old-scope prefix must remain conservative and be
specified and tested in the later implementation; lack of closure evidence cannot
mint a free insertion permission. An old group cannot be continued into the new
scope solely by an unmatched continue/end tag.

## Scope and positive neighbors

- Part scopes are independent. The same technical voice text in another part is
  not a shared group; a boundary in another part is not this part's boundary.
- Only boundaries recognized by the existing lower-domain observer participate.
  An upper-staff octave direction alone does not terminate the lower domain.
- Both starts and stops are boundaries. Equal numeric shift values do not erase
  the event. Visibility filtering does not erase an already recognized boundary.
- A new valid primary-beam beginning after a boundary can establish its own local
  protection. That protection must not be removed by this class.
- A barline, backup, forward, Staff switch, time gap, or intervening attributes
  block is not by itself a member of this class. Their independent policies are
  neither expanded nor retired here.
- Legitimate cross-staff beaming in the same part and scope remains governed by
  existing beam semantics. A Staff change alone is not a reset rule.
- A valid tie-derived clef anchor can predate its physical beam beginning within
  the existing permitted scope. This class does not replace a tie origin with a
  raw note position or change the accepted chord/Unknown contract.
- Unknown or unsupported attachment, membership, boundary or ownership evidence
  remains explicit. A missing relation cannot be converted into write permission.

## Repair and authority stay separate

This definition does not select an implementation or change musical material.
A later repair-scope contract must reference this file byte-unchanged from an
accepted parent. A scope-alignment repair may reconcile the existing consumers'
boundary semantics only within their own part and lower decision domain. It may
not infer source occurrence identity from a voice label, recover withdrawn beam
connections, invent a beam beginning, move octave directions, or reconstruct an
unmatched tie/chord head. Costs, preference weights and independent tie/chord rules
are not authorized to change. Existing normalization, note preservation, bound
receipts, adoption, postconditions and renderer custody remain authoritative.

The implementation must distinguish part solution, normalized plan, actual write,
committed readback and final artifact. A changed solver plan is not proof of a
written or musically approved result. The actual admitted musical effect belongs
in that implementation's manifest and review evidence; this Stage A grants none.

## Required independent probes before productive repair

Positive and negative synthetic cases must cover lower start and stop boundaries,
repeated shift values, an intervening lower note after a boundary, positions before
and after a future boundary, a new group in the new scope, visibility, unchanged
ordinary same-scope groups, upper-only directions, cross-staff groups, backups,
barlines, tie-derived origins, unresolved continuations and independent parts.
Duplicate unrelated parts and groups, rename their labels and relocate them while
preserving local relations: the decisions of original independent groups must stay
unchanged. No global winner, top-N cap or corpus count can grant repair authority.
The real planner, normalization, materializer, receipts/adoption and committed
reentry need their own tests; stubbed transport adapters do not establish them.

## Relation to prior exploratory evidence

Exploratory scans, synthetic scope counterexamples and isolated planner variations
already motivated this definition. They are explicitly not denied or relabelled as
unseen data. No governed classifier consuming this newly frozen definition has
measured its distribution. The protocol's not_yet_measured field refers to that
class distribution, not to the absence of prior exploratory work. Known locations,
counts and solver costs cannot narrow the definition or become its membership
oracle. Normative features above come from the pre-existing hard-scope policy.
Any later corpus evaluation is measurement only and must not reshape the class to
fit a discovered witness.

The ordinary PDF export remains authoritative. Only the successful converter
profile and the exact consumed source are used to obtain the diagnostic
SVG. A disposable copy of its native import marks the corresponding objects
through paint. The native unmarked preview must confirm invariant geometry
and drawing order for that annotation. The bound native shape is matched
one-to-one with the plain committed-source SVG before it gives rise to a
confirmed finding. Complete matching geometry, not pitch, rhythmic position
or spatial proximity alone, supports the correspondence, and multiplicity
ambiguity is reported. Result rows carry no production or veto authority.

An explicit non-beamed chord and its own visible hook and stem prove the
beam-foreign relation. When such evidence is missing the audit does not
promote the candidate to a confirmed overlap. The bounded interior search
provides sufficient witnesses and reports a potentially contacting pair
without a sufficient witness as undecidable. This take does not establish
complete coverage of every SVG or renderer variant. Page counts and the
consumed source remain bound to the successful PDF invocation. No CSS,
MusicXML, PDF, semantic-event or native production data is rewritten.

## Reader preference

An established, unambiguously readable octave line continues to communicate the
same octave display through a staff-local bridge already admitted by the existing
visible register plan. Within that same readable line segment, a fresh generated
word at every bridged reentry is not the preferred default. Retain the initial
orientation indication and the line; omit only the additional generated word when
all the safety evidence below is available. A fresh system/page segment still
requires a readable orientation indication, supplied by the established renderer
restart path where that path is proved.

This is a project engraving preference, supported by Gould's treatment of an
established octave extension line, not a claim that every cautionary repetition
is universally wrong. A continuous-looking PDF alone does not choose a remedy.
The preference applies only after the already accepted semantic scope and bridge
choice are fixed. It supplies no alternative octave span, new duration threshold,
register change, silence merger or event selection.

## Three outcomes for a later implementation

KEEP: a distinct readable orientation indication is required at this entry, or
an independently justified competing/changed reading context requires a reminder.
Preserve the accepted baseline. A new octave display or line start belongs to its
existing owner and cannot be turned into a cue-only omission.

OMIT_ADDITIONAL_GENERATED_WORD: the existing joined scope and exact continuation
are established; their actual source identity is carried into the final owner;
the same final staff domain, octave display and line segment remain established;
a matching orientation indication is readable earlier on that segment; the line
visibly carries that scope across the admitted bridge; and no unresolved competing
line or reading-context change makes the indication ambiguous. Remove only the
word generated for that continuation. Preserve its octave-shift carrier.

UNKNOWN: any required evidence is absent, conflicting, unsupported or stale.
Keep the accepted baseline. Neither the corpus address nor apparent similarity
to a final direction, a low candidate count or a desired PDF can resolve UNKNOWN.

These outcomes are a specification, not executable classification or permission.
The current runtime still uses its unchanged r605 policy and export guards.

## Necessity of the safety restrictions

- The accepted bridge and unchanged semantic scope distinguish the present
  question from octave selection and span repair. Long and short gaps are not
  reclassified here: the existing bridge decision remains the owner.
- Transported continuation origin prevents an output search from inventing known
  identity after projection. The r1517 address census alone is insufficient.
- Same display, domain and line segment preserve the meaning communicated by the
  orientation indication. Numeric labels themselves do not select a remedy.
- The readable preceding orientation indication explains the musical preference:
  a new word would repeat information already carried by that same readable line.
- Supported visible continuity distinguishes an actual line from an XML assertion.
  Normal engraved dashes are not breaks. A new independent gap-size or pixel-distance
  cutoff derived from the observed examples is forbidden.
- Competing lines, changed ownership or an intervening scope ambiguity can destroy
  reader orientation; unresolved cases retain the baseline.
- Final system/page context distinguishes continuation within a segment from a
  fresh segment. A later cue in a restart measure is evaluated at its own entry;
  the measure's restart flag alone cannot decide it.

Other-staff activity neither proves scorewide silence nor independently vetoes
an omission. Examine the actual domain and reading context. Project names, printed
bar labels, absolute pitch/onset/duration, cue spelling, independent multiplicity,
frequency and resemblance to an evaluation example are not repair selectors.

## Owner and guard closure required later

RegisterNotation retains semantic/visible planning. StructuralWriteAuthority
retains the productive mutation entry and the existing final layout writer.
No report, PDF reader, late cleanup pass or proof reentry acquires music authority.

The current early materialization may continue emitting its full baseline reminder.
An eventual final omission must consume a closed, source-bound reader decision at
the existing authorized owner; it may not erase words first and adapt the validator
later. Reconcile that decision with exact materialization, owner receipts/adoption,
final layout, committed XML readback and export validation. Output readback checks
a previously justified transition; it does not mint its origin or permission.

A later implementation contract must reference the unchanged accepted error-class
contract and this separately accepted scope. It must provide an actual decision
path and the transport/epoch binding before authorizing any omission. Merely
relaxing OCTAVE_CONTINUE_RECUE_MISSING or deleting every continue word is forbidden.

## Preserved surfaces and falsification

Only an exactly attributed generated continuation word may change. Notes/rests,
pitches, durations, attacks, beams, ties, slurs, clefs, placements, staff/voice
ownership, octave start/continue/stop topology and line identities remain fixed.
No source/user-authored word is silently included. An unsupported attribution is
UNKNOWN. No new writer or independent ranking/candidate-selection rule is allowed.

Synthetic qualification must cover all four displays; both domain orientations;
unchanged reminders; readable and missing preceding indications; single and
competing lines; short and long admitted gaps without new cutoffs; foreign-staff
activity; starts/stops/display changes; final restarts and later entries in the
same measure; missing/malformed/stale evidence; transported identity; actual
materialization; receipt/adoption; persisted readback; reentry/idempotence; and
renaming, relocation, irrelevant perturbation and independent duplication.

A native full-corpus evaluation must account for every changed word and every
retained/UNKNOWN neighbor. Inspect every rendered page and disclose any secondary
renderer geometry effect. Evaluation addresses and observed counts remain outside
normative tests and product decisions. A failed or incomplete proof retains the
accepted baseline rather than widening the rule.

## Source of the reader principle

Elaine Gould, Behind Bars: The Definitive Guide to Music Notation, sample p. 29,
"Octave signs / for a whole system", publisher-hosted sample:
https://www.behindbarsnotation.co.uk/contents/sample_pages.pdf

Gould explains that an established system-long octave extension line carries
orientation without constant rereading. The cue-only preference and all safety
requirements above are this project's explicit inference and scope, not a
quotation or an assertion that the source prescribes this implementation.

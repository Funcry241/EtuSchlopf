This definition freezes a geometric imbalance class, not a productive selector,
reader, writer, threshold or proof shortcut. Page reduction is outside its
objective. All ordered systems and total pages are preserved. The existing
redundant-page-retention class is not reused as authority for this new objective.

Clearance must use the actual printed outlines and strokes at overlapping
horizontal positions. A negative whole-system vertical-envelope difference can
come from extrema at different horizontal positions and is not a collision.
Beam/stem ownership cannot be inferred solely from nearest system midpoints.
A visible gap is an observation, not an inferred universal minimum.

A future repair must bind the immutable source, the actual consumer and command,
complete submitted and observed partitions, all glyph obligations and explicit
geometry. Unknown geometry, changed bindings, ambiguous component ownership or
unsupported readback must prevent adoption. Source identity travels forward;
PDF resemblance cannot reconstruct it. Existing readability and conservation
gates remain independent. No native successful renderer exit alone authorizes a
replacement. Musical page-turn quality is a separate, potentially binding
obligation and cannot be inferred from a geometrical spacing improvement.

Alternative partitions of the same whole score form one explicit conflict group.
Independent scores share no rank, exclusions, incumbent or write budget. Any
later repair contract must justify locality and independent duplication and refer
to this exact unchanged definition in an already accepted parent. A one-boundary
whole-system transfer is an experimental scope, not a corpus-derived feature of
the error class. No corpus distribution or numerical threshold is frozen here.

The transactional owner retains its existing attempt budget, chord-anchor
guard, individual receipt chain and per-write invariants. A rejected write
leaves the pending decisions on the same bytes. A proven adjacent write
translates only later fragment ranges. When the local evidence is insufficient,
the owner observes the successor before trying another chain. Synthetic checks
exercise independently located chains, a longer chain selected before its
neighbors, ambiguous labels and cursor overlays.

## Meaning and observation boundary

This is a resource-retention error class. It does not classify musical notes,
create a musical norm, decide candidate admissibility or invalidate a release.
An implementation may change storage representation only after proving that
all existing relevant behavior is preserved. Lower storage cost is the intended
difference, not permission to choose another result.

For a retained entry D and its surrounding reachable state S, let pi(D) be a
proposed smaller representation. For every remaining authorized continuation C,
there must be a corresponding continuation C_pi over the projected state such
that:

    Obs(C(S[D])) = Obs(C_pi(S_pi[pi(D)]))

The equation relates correctly typed representations; a full-state reader is
not simply called on an object of another type. Equivalence is contextual and
covers complete future traces, not just one immediate getter or equal totals.
The projection's own construction effects are part of the proof.

Obs includes existing product choices, adopted document bytes, material/source
accounting, identity-bound evidence, final output content, logical error
outcomes, candidate audit rows, order-dependent fingerprints and tie-breaking.
It excludes the intended allocation/retention amounts, measured time and
resource-only outcomes directly caused by storage exhaustion. This exclusion
does not excuse a changed musical choice, skipped evidence, logical error,
search budget, candidate population or explicit stopping criterion.

## Required grounds for a later implementation

1. Inventory every remaining accessor of the retained collection and its entries:
   direct reads, callbacks, parallel workers, moves/removals, later adoption,
   report/fingerprint projection, serialization and aliases outside the container.
   A text search alone is not a proof of complete access coverage.
2. If full consumers exist, bind an exclusion premise that remains true after
   insertion, for every possible later incumbent and authorized continuation.
   Otherwise keep full data or prove a different contextual equivalence. An entry
   not selected in previously observed runs may still be selectable later.
3. Preserve all fields actually consumed by decision keys and final evidence,
   including their exact values and evaluation semantics. Compute the required
   projection at a point where those values remain available and bound. Do not
   manufacture missing source identity or drop a pending obligation as a count.
4. Preserve collection order, multiplicity, index meaning and the incumbent's
   existing equal-key behavior. Names and iteration values may be preserved when
   existing consumers require them; they are not new authority for compaction.
   Reordering entries or choosing a different equal-ranked entry is not justified.
5. Include ownership and destruction effects: shared references, last-owner
   release, custom Drop, reference-count-dependent behavior, evidence registries,
   pending receipts, memoization and identity minting. Data with no later direct
   field read can still have an observable lifetime or destruction effect.
6. Do not modify the eligibility rule, candidate generation, candidate budget,
   ranking, conflict groups, worker count, music transformation or release gate.
   Storage liveness follows existing authority; it does not create selection or
   permission to discard an otherwise selectable result.

## Positive and negative neighbors

- Positive: an immutable existing exclusion makes a retained entry unreachable
  by every full-result consumer; all remaining consumers use exactly the carried
  projection and its destruction effects are observationally neutral.
- Negative: a later consumer can still adopt the entry, mutate the exclusion
  facts, access omitted data through an alias or require its pending evidence.
- Negative: equal-looking summary rows stand in for unequal decision keys,
  losing document access can alter a fallback, or two ordered populations are
  replaced by independently sorted lists.
- Unknown: not all callbacks, aliases, ownership effects or evidence consumers
  have been established. Unknown is not proof that the payload may be removed.

## Invariance and necessity

Renaming or relocating inputs, replacing the musical corpus, changing observed
pool sizes and varying data that remaining consumers cannot observe do not
establish or revoke liveness. Duplicating an independent entry cannot change
another entry's compactability; each duplicated entry retains its own position
and required evidence. Shared-selection or transaction groups must be analyzed
as such, without reinterpreting existing ranking semantics.

Retained positive storage is needed for a retention defect. Complete remaining
access coverage is needed because one missed full consumer refutes projection
sufficiency. Stability is needed whenever consumer exclusion is the argument:
a property that can later change cannot establish permanent nonobservability.
Contextual lifetime/effect equivalence is needed because unreachable fields may
still own resources or evidence observed elsewhere. Preserved ordering is needed
when collection positions or first-match semantics are already observable.

## Later implementation evidence

A future implementation must cite these unchanged bytes from an accepted parent
and justify its exact projection and access graph. It must test both directions:
a fully eligible alternative can still be adopted with identical content, while
an excluded entry that would otherwise win cannot become eligible or lose its
required audit representation. It must test equal-ranked alternatives in their
existing order and independent duplication without changing unrelated outcomes.
Tests must use synthetic data, not fixed corpus identities or observed counts.

For a representation-only implementation, complete native output comparisons
and required candidate/evidence projections must remain unchanged. Resource
measurements compare the same workload and environment and distinguish resident
working set, private/committed storage and paging. A memory model is a prediction,
not a measurement or proof of ownership. No universal capacity threshold or
predicted amount of savings is established by this definition.

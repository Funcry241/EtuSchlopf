## Meaning and objective boundary

The class concerns avoidable retention of pages relative to the agreed objective
of using fewer pages after readability and unchanged musical obligations have
been established. The inherited preference was deliberate: this definition does
not retroactively declare its implementation inconsistent with its old policy.
A later change to that preference would be a real policy transition.

The horizontal system partition is held fixed: the same ordered measure groups
remain the same systems. A change in horizontal grouping, widths, note size,
paper, margins or explicit vertical spacing is outside this first class. The
consumer may redistribute vertical placement when a page assignment changes;
that induced placement is distinct from writing different spacing defaults.
The definition does not require an unchanged first page or any particular count
of systems per page. Such observations of one example are not class features.

The general class follows the geometric packing question: does the extra page
carry a necessary readability or musical obligation when the existing systems
can be placed within the already admitted domain on fewer pages? The negative
observation of unused space alone cannot answer that question. A successful
renderer exit alone cannot answer it either. Unknown source binding, incomplete
readback, unsupported glyph geometry, altered system membership, collisions or
an unresolved binding page-turn obligation leave replacement admissibility open.

## Source, consumer and musical preservation

Every alternative belongs to one whole-score layout transaction. Independent
documents cannot supply ranking, uniqueness, retained renderer exclusions or
write authority to one another. A later policy must be invariant to adding an
independent document with the same situation. Alternatives for the same whole
partition are one explicit conflict group; this is not scorewide ranking of
independent musical occurrences.

The original source and its musical ancestry remain bound. Pitches, attacks,
received durations, voices, staff assignments, rests, ties, notation nodes and
independent directions cannot be changed as a consequence of this definition.
Existing final-source, receipt, materialization, consumer and musical
conservation requirements remain independent. Same-looking PDF symbols do not
establish source identity. Source bytes, not a reconstructed PDF match, must
carry that preservation claim.

A future replacement needs a complete binding to the immutable source, actual
consumer and command profile, submitted full page/system plan and complete
rendered readback. It must preserve the ordered systems and remain inside the
existing frontier and hard capacity/shape limits. Actual fit does not authorize
copied consumer break directives or a source-independent permanent capacity.
Failure or unknown evidence does not license force, an expanded frontier,
smaller notes, a fallback document, omitted notation or a successful verdict.

## Relation to the Schloepfchen formula

The user-approved formulation separates per-measure horizontal and vertical
notation profiles, simultaneous notated heads, system envelopes, page packing
and observed unused space. Readability is a prerequisite. Among admitted
candidates the objective considers page count, excessive expansion and balance
in that order. This definition freezes the page-retention class only; it does
not implement that formula or establish numeric minimum widths and distances.

Already observed glyph envelopes and empty gaps are not intrinsic minima.
Vertical and horizontal available space are different dimensions, and a
full-width staff line does not prove horizontally compact engraving. The
system-height observation must account for upper and lower extents that may
come from different measures. Existing paper, scale and margin policy remains
unchanged. No empirical constant from a real piece becomes a policy parameter.

## Classification, repair and evidence remain separate

This Stage A adds a class definition and Take documentation only. It introduces
no runtime classifier, selector, ranking, threshold, layout writer, reader,
renderer invocation, repair or gate change. It freezes no concrete measure or
page starts and contains no normative real-score fixture.

The preceding isolated final-XML experiment is external feasibility evidence,
not a production implementation or an accepted-parent contract. Its real-score
measurements are not used to define or narrow the class. The general packing
objective and the separation of page assignment from system formation precede
that evaluation. A normative corpus distribution under this frozen class has
not yet been measured; no corpus result is claimed by this definition.

Before any later selector-sensitive implementation, this exact class document
must exist unchanged in an accepted parent. The later implementation contract
must separately justify its repair scope and duplication invariance. The
existing joint Page/System owner must retain decision authority, the existing
materializer must remain the writer and the renderer must remain a feasibility
veto. Existing rejection, receipt, security and Take gates cannot be weakened.
Explicit vertical-spacing changes and horizontal reflow require their own
analysis and admission; no such authority is granted here.

## Meaning and limiting neighbors

This is exclusion of a feasible improving alternative under the declared
objective. It is not a claim that a pitch jump is irrelevant, that every boundary
should be softened, or that a lower scalar cost proves better engraving.
An actual rhythmic-gesture relation is not a prerequisite of this class. The
separate r1509 gesture-context definition remains unchanged and grants no
substitute authority here.

The existing planner chooses at most one contiguous octave window per segment.
Removing boundaries globally can therefore remove valid octave spans elsewhere
in the newly combined segment. Complete baseline coverage is essential; note
preservation alone would not detect the resulting loss of a reading convention.

Every visible object is evaluated under its complete proposed mapping or loco
state. Loco margins and interior low notes cannot be omitted from the alternative's
cost. Existing penalties are charged for complete semantic spans, without
double-counting repeated visual cues. Equal cost does not establish this class.
Different spans, required double-octave displays and a readable loco passage
remain legitimate outcomes. No new preference for 8va is introduced.

Temporal silence and additional binding boundaries are outside the first
pitch-only comparison. An overlapping attack must not acquire an unsafe entry
or exit cut. A tie-unsafe proposal is infeasible regardless of its cost. If a
baseline span crosses the proposed comparison context, its dependencies must
first be closed; otherwise membership remains UNKNOWN, not positive.

## Necessity review

| Feature | Why necessary | Limiting neighbor |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pitch-only search exclusion | Establishes this pruning mechanism | An independently required temporal or ownership fence |
| Same-state feasible common proposal | Distinguishes a lost alternative from a prohibited one | Unsafe tie, anchor, overlap or written-pitch state |
| Complete common context | Makes costs comparable | A cropped window that hides a worse loco margin |
| Baseline octave coverage retained | Prevents sacrificing another high island | A locally cheaper window that drops another existing span |
| Strict existing-objective improvement | Supplies an independent reason to compare | Equal cost, changed weights or preference for a known result |

The numerical jump threshold in the current implementation is historical policy,
not a new class-defining constant. Likewise, the existing four-object eligibility
rule and costs are not relaxed to manufacture an eligible short tail.

## Evidence and falsification performed before this definition

The exact r1510 input captures reproduced all ten native plan hashes. This binds
the investigation to the real planning phase, not a final-XML approximation.
An earlier endpoint example motivated investigation; it is not held out evidence
and does not supply a permanent selection address.

An isolated experiment using the actual accepted parser and planner falsified
blanket pitch-boundary removal: two high islands separated by a readable low
passage lost the second octave span, and the existing cost rose from 0 to 148.
All five topology counters still remained zero. Structural green alone therefore
does not establish preservation of the octave choices.

A narrower read-only comparison passed 13 synthetic expectations: a beneficial
common span; its time-scaled/renamed version and independent-part duplication;
separate high islands with scaled and duplicated variants; a true low/high
excursion; long silence; an incoming tie; necessary double octave; readable low
tail; below-minimum material; and a sounding overlap. The long-silence case still
improved material on one side, but did not bridge the rest. The overlap case
admitted a common enclosing span, without putting a boundary inside the held tone.
These are isolated diagnostic results, not native repair or rendered acceptance.
No prospective governed classifier has measured this class's corpus distribution.

## Obligations after acceptance, before productive writing

1. Implement a read-only membership/comparison witness for a closed local context.
   Bind exact input and baseline plan hashes. Keep explicit negative and UNKNOWN
   outcomes. Do not use scorewide ranking, first-only limits or corpus cardinality.
2. Exercise every baseline span, local candidate conflict and external dependency.
   Duplication of independent material must leave each local decision unchanged.
   Extend the synthetic probes to hidden/chord carriers, mixed durations,
   outgoing tie continuations, context-crossing spans, visibility and supported
   upper/lower display domains. Unsupported relations stay UNKNOWN.
3. Measure the frozen class over the ten real captured inputs. Distribution may
   test breadth and find counterexamples; it may not narrow the class to a desired
   measure, octave kind, source label or pitch pattern.
4. Only a separately justified repair scope may replace a baseline choice. Keep
   RegisterNotationPlan, the existing visibility projection, central writer,
   receipts, adoption and committed reentry. Verify actual materialization and
   rendered notation, with no regression hidden by a lower aggregate cost.

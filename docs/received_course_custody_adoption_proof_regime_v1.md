## Status and meaning

This KodexOnly parent fixes the proof requirement before a later productive
implementation. Acceptance of this parent does not install a runtime gate or
prove the present Render path complete. Every existing runtime, owner, receipt,
selector, validator, TrustAnchor, build profile and test remains byte-identical
to accepted r1448. A later child may not amend this regime while implementing it.
Any implementation blocked by an existing construction pin or governance rule
needs its own preceding accepted, bounded governance transition; this document
provides no executable exception and no generic permission to alter those pins.

The referenced error class and semantic contracts remain unchanged. This is a
proof regime for continuing already authenticated received-course identities and
obligations into adopted product states. It creates no new musical obligation,
canonical pitched role, course, rest responsibility, channel assignment or write
capability. Missing proof is Unknown, not proof of musically incorrect output.
After runtime activation, however, Unknown cannot authorize the affected
productive adoption or release.

`require_complete_course_context` is a prerequisite for this transport, not a
terminal fulfilment certificate. A successful transport must preserve the whole
received identity/ancestry domain, including existing rest obligations and
reparameterized support. It must not declare those obligations discharged merely
because their context is complete. Final fulfilment and legitimate terminal
discharge remain with the unchanged obligation contract and final gate.

## Authoritative roots and carried state

The selected sovereign Master supplies its existing authenticated custody, bound
to its exact document and actual lineage epoch. Neither a serialized Master nor
a Render artifact can create a replacement identity root. Missing selected
custody, an earlier Unknown, stale byte binding or incomplete received course
context is not an authenticated empty domain. An empty domain is valid only when
the original authenticated custody itself proves it empty.

Every productive Master-to-Render genesis and admitted Master substitution must
consume custody from that same selected Master epoch. Early and late Master
epochs remain distinct. The existing issuer, epoch, history-position and freshness
checks stay mandatory; custody for equal-looking or unrelated material cannot
replace them. The late epoch's existing checked temporal proof remains mandatory
and unchanged. No final XML scan may mint the missing Master custody.

Each adopted Render state must carry both its sovereign document/lineage and the
continuation of that authoritative root. A later state-root transition continues
that state's custody. The historical #58 path continues the exact admitted #58
state, subject to its unchanged freshness and DirectionRelocation restrictions.
An authorized Master substitution carries its explicitly admitted Master root;
it may not silently reset an Unknown on an ordinary Render-state transition or
erase obligations whose existing contract requires continued accounting.

## Original operations, not reconstructed correspondence

Continuation uses the original owners' actual operations, opaque execution
identities, creation captures and certified ancestry in application order. A
receipt's byte replay alone proves the edit happened, not the musical course
correspondence. Carrying only an owner label, population row or before/after
fingerprint is insufficient. The actual capture must travel with its receipt;
losing it in an adapter must remain detectable rather than becoming success.

Whole-document, fragment, generated-fragment, enclosing and instruction-source
receipt placements must retain their real roles. Inner/enclosing events cannot
be counted twice as new births, and an instruction-only event cannot advance
custody as though it rewrote the product document. A fragment's original ranges
locate carried identities in the bound parent epoch; they do not establish
identity from position. Unsupported placement or absent original capture refuses
the proof. Later XML matching by pitch, staff, voice, onset, duration, order or
similarity must never reconstruct missing identity, ancestry or responsibility.

Exact unchanged bytes may retain an already valid, exactly bound custody object
without minting a receipt, identity or event. Byte equality cannot create custody
where none existed. A notation-only edit still owes its original permitted
transport; unchanged sounding projections do not exempt that boundary.

## Trial construction and productive adoption

An isolated candidate or read-only probe may fail continuation and record its
first Unknown without changing any selected or persisted product state. A
verified losing candidate closes only its own pending receipts. Its failure does
not invalidate an independent complete candidate or the unchanged selected root.
It must never contaminate or heal another candidate's custody. Independent
duplication, order or number of losing candidates must not change the result for
a given candidate and root.

Candidate construction is not productive selection. Before a candidate becomes
the selected Master, before a Master is exported as released product, and before
any Render candidate reaches a productive artifact sink, its complete continuation
must be proven from its actual root. A chosen candidate with Unknown cannot be
adopted, published, substituted with unchecked bytes, relabelled as success or
used as the basis of downstream work. Failure of a selected productive transition
propagates as failure through the existing project and batch result path. It is
not a new musical ranking, automatic repair or permission to try an unchecked
fallback. Ordinary preselection trials and their existing independent selection
rules remain distinct from this refusal.

The Render proof precedes the first productive file write, not merely the final
lineage commit. Path-bound preparation must produce the final planned bytes and
their complete owner transport before the sink can write. Any sink that performs
additional owned edits, including the existing #58 checkpoint owner, must first
prepare those edits and their original capture; checking them only after physical
write is too late. Existing sink restrictions, original receipt mints, byte replay,
artifact readback and lineage commit checks remain independently mandatory.

A refused proof leaves the selected document, custody, lineage, checkpoint and
productive artifact unchanged. In-place candidate construction must therefore
stay isolated until verification succeeds. The prepared proof is opaque and
binds the exact issuer, root epoch, owner execution chain and final candidate
bytes; a caller-created audit, cloned boolean, string label or proof for A cannot
authorize B. Commit consumes that proven transition once, together with the
matching readback. Failure is never recorded as a successful no-op.

## Causal and authority review

The boundary is determined by movement into selected/persisted product state,
not by the location of a known failure. Required ancestry and coverage come from
the original authenticated domain and unchanged contracts. Proof completeness
prevents a vanished identity from falling outside the checked image. Binding to
the exact root and execution prevents a valid unrelated proof from being reused.
Checking before the sink prevents a refused candidate from already becoming the
product. Separating trials prevents an unselected experiment from becoming a
scorewide release veto. None of these premises depends on corpus distribution.

Project, etude, filename, measure/page/system number, pitch, register, spelling,
numeric staff/voice/channel/source labels, owner frequency, renderer capacity,
conversion success, revision timing and candidate count cannot select this
proof requirement or justify relaxing it. Existing complete independent groups
remain independent. No global ranking or scorewide multiplicity grants authority.

Synthetic tests must exercise actual receipt/capture adapters and actual adoption
entry points, rather than constructing a successful audit. They must cover:

- Complete received roots; explicitly authenticated empty roots; absent custody,
  sticky first Unknown, incomplete context and stale document/epoch/issuer.
- Early/late genesis, current-state roots, admitted substitutions and the #58
  historical path; a proof for equal bytes under a foreign origin must fail.
- Every productive sink shape, including publication callbacks, path-bound
  preparation and additional #58 edits; on refusal no productive callback runs
  and document, artifact, lineage, custody and checkpoint stay unchanged.
- Original exact, block, additive and preserving transports; missing capture,
  foreign/reused execution, unsupported fragment placement, nested owners and
  instruction-only events; no invented birth or geometry-derived ancestry.
- Valid unchanged-byte continuation and invalid missing-custody no-op; a proof
  for A cannot authorize B; one successful proof cannot authorize two commits.
- Losing Unknown candidates leave their complete selected root and independent
  candidates intact; selecting an Unknown candidate refuses before publication.
  Renaming, independent duplication and trial order preserve local outcomes.
- Existing rest obligations and reparameterized support survive every successful
  transition without implicit discharge; complete context alone cannot pay them.

Tests must use synthetic original creation and execution evidence, not captured
corpus locations or a reconstructed identity fixture. All existing receipt,
sovereignty, custody, rest and beam tests remain mandatory. No test, validator,
TrustAnchor, gate order, toolchain, profile or optimization may be weakened.
Both parent-compiled admission and admission of the actual extracted take are
required, including the controlled installed transaction backup shape.

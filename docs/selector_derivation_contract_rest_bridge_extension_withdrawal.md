## Repair scope

Only a writer-authored, adopted and continuously validated E050 extension of a
complete original positive group can be withdrawn. Each positive group is retained
from its actual owner before source-sharing components are assembled; its explicit
component association is inherited, never recovered from diagnostic names. The actual
simultaneous late voice assignment must separate the carried connection while
keeping its entire original source-sharing component together. Restore exactly the beam states that this operation
replaced, with the same source/attack/fragment identities and complete chord
resources. All source identities predate the operation; a current XML group or
an Origin-Census difference cannot author an extension record.

Missing custody, conflicting/overlapping operations, generated or duplicated
resources, changed relevant beams, timing or attachments decline the optional
repair or reject a required stale transition. No voice, role or original
membership is changed. The supported owner operation class is exact preservation
of the ordered pitched image plus unchanged participating connection resources;
enharmonic respelling may preserve MIDI identity. FinalChord handover uses its
actual typed materialization provenance, with complete unique fragment images.
Removed connections never regain evidence. Each ordinary transition is checked,
including inactive/no-op paths; this is not a whitelist of observed writer names.

Independent complete connections have independent local decisions. Batch footprints
must be disjoint; overlap is fail-closed without choosing a first winner. Known
corpus coordinates do not occur in the selector. Synthetic evidence must exercise
adoption/discard, foreign sources, stale pre-states, timing and beam drift,
complete chords, renaming/relocation and independent duplication.

## Complete resource image inside source-sharing components

The connection consists only of the writer-selected heads and their complete
attached chord resources. Additional original component occurrences, all fragments
of each authenticated source/attack and their chord cohorts are closed to a fixed
point as read-only resources. Missing bound fragments decline capture. Resources
never vote on connection separation and never become restoration rows. Their voices
must remain unchanged in ordinary preservation steps or equal their authenticated
sealed target labels in the actual FinalChord projection.

Read-only resources may be shared across independently authored connections. Each
connection validates its entire member/resource image; a changed resource retires
every dependent connection. Restoration footprints must be disjoint; overlap is
a hard error. Global pitched sequence validation remains strict. Local beam drift
retires dependent connections without retiring independent ones. Any change to
the global voice criterion requires a failing end-to-end test demonstrating the
need and preserves all other global checks.

This is an authority/safety scope correction within the unchanged accepted parent
error class, not a new error class or an early bridge-admission decision.

## Observation boundary and necessity

This is a diagnostic reporting-gap class, not a new musical defect classifier. Scope-local unmatched markers may involve information beyond that scope and are not automatically invalid music; cross-voice serialization is not proof of lost required beam continuity. The observation scope, supported beam level and units must be printed explicitly. The canonical group/notation proofs remain with the existing accepted beam error classes. Consumer reservations remain defined by the unchanged accepted partwide renderer channel-capacity profile.

Later read-only counting may report primary begin/continue/end balance per part/measure/voice and the number of unreserved channel/staff reservations. It may not create a writer, change simulation, allocator, report verdict, issue severity, repair target or release veto. Missing/invalid input must remain unavailable rather than becoming zero. A part/measure-local scope must not claim to validate cross-scope beam semantics. Independent duplicated scopes contribute additively; opaque renaming preserves counts. Rest bridges and cross-staff same-voice chains must not be broken by the counting algorithm. No classifier or counters are installed in this Stage A.

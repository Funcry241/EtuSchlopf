## Causal meaning and limits

An octave-display boundary is a change of reading convention. A visible primary
beam can establish a rhythmic gesture whose attacks are read together. Neither
property alone is an error. The class is the omission of an independently bound
relation from the decision that places a boundary inside that relation.

Class membership establishes incomplete decision context, not that the resulting
notation is necessarily worse. A true register excursion can justify an internal
octave change. A continuous octave line can produce worse low notes, excess ledger
lines, a different tie interpretation, or invalid materialization. These remain
counterevidence, not exceptions to be removed to recover a desired example.

A claimed existing gesture must be bound to the same actual input state and
phase as the proposed display decision. If that evidence is absent, ambiguous,
stale, or only available after the decision, the relation is UNKNOWN. It is not a
proven group, a prohibition, a candidate ranking, or permission to write. Evidence
availability may require a separate prerequisite before a productive repair.

The definition does not authorize copying a final rendered beam grouping back
into an earlier musical authority, inventing a group from onset or voice-label
equality, reconstructing withdrawn beam connections, or moving an existing phase.
Secondary beams, hook orientation, chord membership, and cross-staff projection
cannot silently replace the existing primary-group ownership contract.

## Necessity review

| Fact | Independent necessity | Limiting neighbor |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Register-mapping boundary | Without a mapping change this particular continuity question does not arise | A visible repeated 8va cue with unchanged state is not a new boundary |
| Bound shared gesture across it | The relation supplies the context that temporal separation alone lacks | Separate groups or an independently established gesture boundary are different cases |
| Same-state, same-phase evidence | Otherwise later notation would become retroactive musical authority | A final PDF can motivate investigation but cannot bind an earlier planner input |
| Relation omitted from the decision | This distinguishes missing context from a deliberate, justified internal switch | A decision that considers the group and retains a necessary change is not this omission |

An octave label is scoped by its actual part and display domain, not by a numeric
staff label treated as identity. A cross-staff group requires an explicit account
of which events share that domain. Hidden chord members and structural carriers
must retain their existing ownership and tie obligations; they are not extra
visible reading attacks merely because they occur in the source.

## Repair is a separate question

A later classifier may expose a bound relation or UNKNOWN while keeping every
existing productive decision unchanged. A later repair scope must separately
prove a complete feasible alternative and its authority. It must account for the
full surrounding unchanged display state, actual clefs, written spelling, visible
ledger burden, internal state transitions, incoming and outgoing ties, chord
attachment, hidden events, and existing materialization limits.

Maximum and aggregate ledger burden and the number of reading-state transitions
are useful independently inspectable comparisons; this document installs no
weight, priority order, hard maximum, optimizer, or acceptance threshold for them.
A reduced transition count alone is insufficient. A uniformly applied 8va, a ban
on 15ma, or a blanket ban on changes inside beams is not authorized.

Any future musical choice remains owned by RegisterNotationPlan. Its existing
semantic-plan to display-state to StructuralWriteAuthority to receipt/adoption
path remains intact. The writer may not choose a different span to make the PDF
look better. Sounding notes, onset/duration, note visibility, beam membership, ties,
and clef policy acquire no new mutation permission through this definition.
The accepted incoming-tie safety contract and lower-clef octave-scope contract
remain separate, unchanged constraints.

## Independent probe obligations before implementation

Use synthetic examples covering the same pitches and times with different bound
group relations; a group-preserving time and measure relocation; renamed technical
labels; duplicated independent groups and parts; different spellings and register
placements that preserve the relevant comparisons; hidden and upper/lower chord
hosts; incoming and outgoing ties; same-state cues versus real start/stop events;
an actually necessary internal register change; a justified double-octave span;
cross-staff groups; unequal durations and temporal overlap; and missing or stale
group evidence. Unsupported relations remain explicitly UNKNOWN.

Membership and any later local decision must be invariant under duplication of
unrelated independent material. No scorewide winner, first-only repair, top-N cap,
rarity test, or candidate count grants authority. A shared conflict group must be
explicitly established, not inferred from a common piece name.

Real planner-input capture, canonical group ownership, normalization, complete
span materialization, receipts/adoption, and committed reentry require actual
integration evidence. Endpoint arithmetic or a mocked writer is insufficient.

## Exploratory history

The earlier score review and arithmetic register comparisons motivated this work.
They are not being claimed as unseen evidence. The definition above is derived
from the mapping and grouping relation, not from a concrete measure, pitch, span
count, or ledger threshold that isolates an example. No governed classifier for
this newly frozen class has measured its corpus distribution; that is the meaning
of not_yet_measured. Known engraving examples belong to evaluation and may not
subsequently narrow this definition.

## Derivation

The accepted parent lineage already freezes `visible_rest_elision_without_bound_rhythmic_dispensability_proof`.
The affected owner materializes only one kind of productive delta for a selected visible rest: it replaces the complete carrier by identity-neutral cursor time. A proof for that operation must therefore bind the complete carrier interval.

The pre-r1181 predicate used summed other-voice overlap and accepted a carrier once that sum reached one half of the rest duration. Summation is not a coverage proof: concurrent pitches can increase the sum while the union of represented time remains unchanged. Even without multiplicity, a strict subset of the carrier interval leaves visible rhythmic time for which this owner has no bound dispensability evidence.

r1181 is intentionally conservative. It does not add an interval splitter to this early raw-voice owner and it does not infer musical lane identity from raw voice labels. Instead, whole-carrier eligibility now requires the already-computed union coverage to cover the entire rest interval. Partial cases remain unchanged at this stage and may be handled later only by an owner with the required canonical binding.

The later FinalChord visible-rest interval projection already owns interval splitting after canonical voice binding. Keeping a partial carrier here therefore preserves authority ordering rather than creating a second splitter.

## Multiplicity and neighboring cases

The decision depends only on one carrier duration and the union of concrete overlap intervals within that carrier. Duplicating a simultaneous pitched event does not enlarge the union and therefore cannot create write authority. Duplicating an independent rest candidate cannot change another carrier's local union coverage.

The existing fully covered synthetic neighbor remains eligible. The existing partial-overlap test is extended in place to include a majority-but-incomplete overlap and remains ineligible. The existing multiplicity readback test is extended in place to assert that three simultaneous overlaps covering only one quarter of the interval do not become a whole-rest candidate.

No new writer-reaching test function, writer root, StructuralWriteAuthority surface, scorewide ranking, action budget, or corpus-conditioned selector is introduced.

## Scope limit

This take removes only the pre-existing whole-rest authority for incomplete union coverage in the early unbound FinalVisibleRestRole fallback. It does not claim that complete raw-voice coverage is a universal musical dispensability proof, does not alter the frozen-voice bound selector, and does not change later FinalChord interval projection.

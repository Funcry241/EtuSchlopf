# Continuation refusal byte evidence — r1384

Parent: accepted r1383. RefactorOnly; no musical change or new refusal condition.
Hardware problems are reported resolved by the user. No hardware attribution is
made by this take, and no cause is inferred from historical failed runs.

The existing foreign-representation-post-state and physical-course-note-support
refusals keep their exact predicates, errors and fatal propagation. Only their
failure messages gain original expected/observed byte lengths, SHA-256, first
byte difference and bounded hexadecimal context. UTF-8 is never sliced as text.
The comparison consumes the actual strings already compared at the refusal;
it creates no origin, receipt, continuation, role, selection, recovery or writer
permission. Evidence is computed only on the original failure branch. Successful
paths do not hash, compare again, allocate evidence or change their result.

The original-byte audit module owns the formatter. Existing real octave receipt
and transport tests verify foreign-state refusal and unchanged positive reuse.
A synthetic actual MIDI-seed receiver verifies a changed note is still refused,
its originals remain bound, and a later valid call still succeeds. Byte-context
checks cover Unicode, truncation and bounded output. Real corpus inputs remain
evaluation only. Acceptance requires fresh Windows 10/10 corpus, unchanged XMLs,
PDF pages and sovereign music lineage relative to r1383. This is failure evidence
hardening, not a claimed repair of past crashes or a performance optimization.

The isolated Raw-Splice harness compiles the real original-byte formatter module
and the real shared SHA implementation. No stub, receipt replacement or relaxed
assertion is supplied; its actual receipt core and checks remain unchanged.

## Closed scope and existing authority

The supported piano lower domain is the existing staff-2 domain, not a learned
identity selector. Complete unchanged upper proposals and all surrounding lower
notes are retained. The original register owner retains its complete event IDs,
source bytes and semantic proposal; the original creator invokes the comparison
before materializing any direction. No source identity is reconstructed from a
later score, approximate pitch matching or output geometry.

Alternative hypotheses are complete versions of one part in one transaction,
not independent local writes. Contiguous sets of existing complete register
spans may be omitted. This materialization limitation needs no new direction
anchor, split scope or repair boundary. Disjoint parts are independently decided.
Equal/worse courses and unsupported contexts preserve the incumbent. In addition
to strict reduction of the existing scalar cost, the combined count of actual
clef transitions and true octave-mapping changes must strictly decrease. This
separate reader preference implements the user's confirmed calm-reading goal;
it is not an error-class condition or a new numerical weight. Continue reminders
are reported separately and do not count as mapping changes. No octave
ban, treble preference, ledger ceiling or new cost constant is introduced.

Immutable preparation reuses the original octave direction preparer and original
lower-clef measure preparer with their actual carried state. Only numerical
complete-course measurements escape. The prepared XML cannot become an adopted
source, cannot mint a receipt and cannot issue creator origins. The selected
semantic proposal is materialized through the same original octave creator,
BLOCK-50 authority, original direction-origin issuance and existing adoption.
The original lower-clef and later clef materializers stay responsible for clefs.
No new writer reachability, late cleaner or alternative public sink is authorized.

Current raw note fragments, spelling, staff, voice, visibility, onset, duration,
rests, chords, ties, beams and slurs receive no mutation permission. Existing
receipts and final artifact checks remain required. Synthetic positive/negative,
necessary high/double-octave, complete margins, ties, chords, hidden notes,
renaming, time/measure relocation and independent duplication are required.
Full current corpus and final rendered quality are evaluation, not class inputs.

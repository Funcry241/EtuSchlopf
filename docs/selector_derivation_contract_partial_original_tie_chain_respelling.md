## Meaning and independent derivation

A tie represents continuation of an already sounding note. Changing the spelling
of only a proper subset of its original connected chain can leave differently
spelled endpoints on an unchanged sounding continuation. This violates the
existing complete-chain engraving rule even when every MIDI pitch is preserved.
The class follows from that rule, not from the distribution of a known example.
It applies to any length of chain, including internal occurrences carrying both
incoming and outgoing ties, and to chord members and hidden occurrences.

## Necessity and exclusions

Without original tie connectivity, independently respelled notes need not share
one spelling. Without a spelling change this is not a respelling failure. With
equal resulting written pitches across every original edge, a complete-chain
respelling is outside the failure class. A separately authorized spelling
transition is governed by its own contract and is not silently inferred here.
Changed sounding pitch, support, staff/course presentation or disposition is
not licensed as enharmonic respelling and requires its own existing authority.

Original connectivity must already be bound or carried from its original owner.
Output pitch, onset, staff, voice labels or proximity cannot recreate missing
identity. Absent, ambiguous or incomplete chain evidence remains a refusal to
authorize a repair; it is not permission to choose an endpoint or invent ties.

## Invariance and later implementation requirements

Renaming projects, measures, voices and source identifiers, transposing a valid
synthetic example while retaining its enharmonic relation, moving it in time,
or scaling duration units preserves the causal definition. Adding or duplicating
an independent chain does not change membership or authorization of another.
No global first candidate, scorewide uniqueness or action cap is authorized.

A later implementation must cite this unchanged file from an accepted parent,
separately define its repair scope and prove local authority at the existing
respelling owner. It must treat a complete original connected chain as one
consistent decision, refuse unresolved or conflicting chain evidence, and
preserve sounding content, support, presentation, tie edges and existing
receipt/adoption requirements. No particular replacement spelling is selected
by this definition. Synthetic original-owner probes must include complete and
partial chains, internal and chord occurrences, missing/ambiguous evidence,
renaming, relocation and independent duplication. Korpus measurements cannot
narrow the class or authorize a write.

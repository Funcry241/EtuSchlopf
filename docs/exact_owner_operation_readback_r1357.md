## Demonstrated prerequisite

The remaining unforced importer failure needs complete origin-bound occurrence,
course, role and musical-run accounting before bounded channel reuse. The current

signatures for its existing source relation. Those matches cannot provide new

## Existing authority, unchanged decisions

`ExactTransformationReceipt::operation_readback` borrows the original receipt and
replays its original script against its bound pre-state. The old replay method
now delegates to this reader. Existing input/output fingerprint binding, script
realization, receipts, mint capabilities, owner signatures, SSC adoption and
musical decisions are unchanged. No registry, frozen contract or gate is amended.

The new reader carries a caller-bound existing range through the original edits.
Changes wholly inside that range and whole-range replacements are accounted in
its extent; insertions at its beginning precede it, insertions at its end follow
it. Cross-boundary edits, out-of-domain or empty input ranges and completely
removed outputs refuse range continuation. Such refusal does not discharge any
obligation. Replay's unedited byte spans use this same primitive and must match
exactly. This introduces no new accepted or rejected replay states: realization
of a valid exact script necessarily conserves all of its unedited spans.

Range transport supplies locations only. It issues no origin, course, role,
conservation verdict, omission witness, new birth or adoption permission. A
replaced note's changed musical obligations still require its actual owner;
an inserted note needs actual creation capture. Readback cannot mint later at a
past creation event. Its pre-existing-range result is not a complete inventory.
The borrowed receipt remains necessary independently at sovereign adoption.

# Canonical rest context: authority before materialization

Contract: canonical_rest_context_v1
Status: definition only; no productive issuer, role inference or writer added.
ChangeClass: KodexOnly
Parent: accepted r1347, version 0.10.1839.

## Problem and boundaries

The accepted role_occurrence_binding_v1 contract requires independently
authorized roles for all role-sensitive occurrences. It does not authorize
deriving a rest role from an export channel. r1347 transports exact physical
non-pitched continuations, with role Unknown, through FinalChord projection.
It supplies neither creation identity nor musical continuity.

The current VisibleRhythmLedger creates gap rests before final canonical role
formation. Its creation inputs are time, staff, an XML voice label and rhythm
context. Its lineage rows cover surviving source fragments, not newly inserted
gap rests. A whole-block transformation receipt binds the actual transformation
but does not establish an individual rest's role or musical responsibility.
Final canonical source-role formation currently covers pitched source classes.
FrozenSoundingTemporalLanePlan supplies positive sounding evidence, not negative
rest responsibility; temporal coloring is not a musical voice-run decision.

This definition addresses missing authority at that boundary. It supplements
role_occurrence_binding_v1 and leaves the accepted partwide renderer capacity
class and all existing causal contracts unchanged. It does not declare every
existing pause wrong or establish that a capacity repair is feasible.

## Three independent facts

1. Occurrence identity answers which physical representation continues which
   creation event or already-bound input. It carries no musical decision.
2. Musical continuity answers which explicitly authorized musical course owns
   an occurrence or obligation. Equal roles, channels, adjacent geometry,
   temporal colors and HARD SAME chord membership do not establish a course.
3. A role segment answers the independently authorized presentation role for
   an interval of that course. A course may have several explicit role or staff
   segments. Role equality cannot merge courses; course equality cannot invent
   a missing role or staff assignment.

Opaque identities distinguish events even with equal geometry. They are minted
only at an admitted owner creation event and transported through exact receipts.
Their numeric values cannot determine membership, priority, role or permits.
XML offsets locate physical evidence in one exact transaction; they cannot mint
or reconstruct any of these identities.

## Two origins, different obligations

Existing imported, retained or restified material must retain its actual origin
through normalization, splitting, projection and rendering. An existing visible
rest is already a represented rhythmic obligation. Missing independent role or
continuity remains Unknown and cannot justify deleting it, rebinding it by label
or treating it as newly generated silence. A restified pitched source keeps its
source/disposition ancestry but needs an explicit rest-context decision; pitch
loss alone does not supply that decision.

New gap rests require an explicit coverage declaration from the canonical
musical owner. A coverage declaration is a positive musical decision that a
specified course must be rhythmically represented over a specified interval,
with explicit role segments, presentation-staff segments and visibility policy.
Neither the first/last note of a measure nor its total duration supplies that
declaration. A declaration cannot be issued merely because a technical lane has
a gap or the renderer needs fewer labels.

The proposed declaration is NOT an existing capability in r1347. A later change
must admit its issuer and the musical decision that creates the course and its
coverage. This document cannot turn an arbitrary caller-supplied assertion,
configuration value, XML grouping or successful audit into owner authority.
If no independently authorized declaration is available, the new path is Unknown;
another identity-only transport Take does not close this prerequisite.

## Frozen negative-rest context

Before authorizing a new rest, a sealed context must bind:

- the exact owner decision and pre-state, canonical course identity and explicit
  coverage interval;
- a complete lineage-bound inventory of represented activity and existing rest
  obligations for that course throughout the coverage interval;
- explicit role, staff and visibility segments that uniquely cover the interval;
- a separate complete positive sounding view, including hidden sounding material,
  for conflict checks; its absence alone grants no silence authority;
- the supported metric realization policy and protected host, tie and cursor
  obligations.

Only within an authorized coverage declaration, with complete and conflict-free
bound activity, may its unrepresented interval become a new rest obligation.
The formal difference is `licensed coverage minus represented interval union`,
not `measure time minus notes found under an XML label`. Existing rests occupy
that union and retain their separate identity; they are not recreated or merged
away. Hidden pitched material is not silent and must participate in the bound
activity/conflict proof. Grace, zero-duration and unpitched objects retain their
separate structural obligations and cannot be assumed to consume positive time.

Each resulting interval must be split at explicit role/staff/visibility boundaries
and at the existing metric-policy boundaries. Boundary transport, cross-staff
continuation or measure segmentation never infers a new role. A hidden carrier
or Forward has the parent obligation's lineage, plus its distinct structural
carrier obligations; this does not make its interval a visible-rest obligation.
Unsupported or multiply covered role/staff segments, unknown dispositions,
incomplete inventory or a positive sounding conflict refuse the affected new
mutation. Other independently complete groups retain their own decisions.

The context issues a rest obligation, not a free-standing XML writer permit.
The existing structural owner must independently authorize its realization,
including visibility and exact host/cursor preservation. Cursor fillers and
musical visible rests remain different obligations even when durations coincide.

## Phase order and closed implementation boundary

The required dependency order is:

1. Freeze final sounding source/disposition truth and the canonical musical
   course/coverage decisions. The latter need their own admitted authority;
   SourceEventId, HARD SAME and temporal lanes do not supply them automatically.
2. Obtain the existing canonical presentation-role decisions from their current
   admitted prerequisites. Bind explicit course role/staff segments without
   rerunning role inference on rest geometry or channel labels.
3. Freeze complete existing-rest ancestry and licensed negative-rest contexts.
4. Materialize newly authorized obligations at the existing structural owner,
   capturing creation-event identity and actual fragment receipts at birth.
5. Carry every represented occurrence through all later writers, preserving
   splits as 1:N relations and requiring explicit authority for any N:1 merge.
6. Migrate every direct and indirect role-sensitive consumer to exact occurrences.
   Musical-run grouping also migrates independently; a role lookup change alone
   cannot make `(staff, voice)` aggregation safe for mixed-role shared channels.
7. Only then attempt universal, complete-part channel reuse under the unchanged
   capacity class, with exact final persisted/native consumer readback.

This is a dependency contract, not permission to reorder current passes blindly.
The current role owner depends on canonical pitched written projection. That
prerequisite must remain available without depending on the proposed final rest
realization or a compacted channel choice. A later implementation must show the
actual acyclic call/data path; it may not fabricate an early role from a temporal
lane to break the cycle. Existing imported rests are preserved while their
authority is bound. Staging a physical ledger proposal does not commit it or
grant its proposed rests roles. No obligation is adopted by matching a later
role to an earlier XML label.

The complete implementation is a musical authority change, not merely RefactorOnly.
This Take admits no new capability and updates no canonical writer allowlist.
Its implementation must pass the existing governance gates with its true change
class. No special permission, force fallback or gate weakening follows from
accepting this document.

## Preservation, SSC and acceptance

SSC can carry an already authorized origin, exact pre-state, proof and continuation
through admitted boundaries. A receipt proves the actual edit occurred. Neither
SSC, receipt existence nor a WouldPermit observation establishes a missing
musical course, role segment or negative-rest obligation.

Actual-owner synthetic tests must prove creation capture, complete input coverage,
exact 1:N continuation, distinct coincident rests, preserved restified-source
ancestry and role/staff segment transport. They must exercise actual rest/beam
consumer and typed writer boundaries, including host/tie/cursor negative cases.
Missing, conflicting, stale or forged contexts must refuse the affected new path
without converting Unknown to an allowed role or changing current released music
as a side effect of adding observation. No broad new global release veto is
authorized by this definition.

Project/measure renaming, opaque-ID bijections, channel-label renaming, role/staff
permutation with full authority transport and uniform time scaling preserve
decisions. Independent musical groups are additive. Duplicate observations are
idempotent; duplicating musical material creates distinct obligations and can
change resource demand within its shared group. No scorewide uniqueness, global
ranking, fixed voice count, pitch/register heuristic or corpus-derived feature
may issue responsibility or write permission.

Before claiming closure, inventory all producers and role/run-sensitive readers,
prove exact persisted master/render occurrence coverage including rests, forwards,
hosts and ties, then run ordinary unforced consumer conversion and exact native
readback. A PDF or zero exit code alone is insufficient. This contract does not
remove existing accepted rest-dispensability rules. Any later rule that uses
shared channels must establish that its proof still distinguishes the original
musical responsibilities; old label equivalence cannot be silently reused.

The executable model in canonical_rest_context_foundation_r1348.md checks only
the conditional obligation algebra under supplied, assumed-authorized premises.
It does not prove a productive authority issuer, its musical choices, full input
closure, output preservation or bounded channel feasibility.

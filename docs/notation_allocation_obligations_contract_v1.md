1. Each represented occurrence has an exact carried identity and independently
   justified notation obligations. The domain includes pitched and non-pitched
   carriers, chord heads and members, tied or split continuations, visible and
   hidden rests and original cursor obligations. Several fragments of one attack
   remain distinct occurrences. The source-level role table currently present
   in the product supplies only its established source-level evidence.

2. Sounding occupancy and written occupancy retain separate types and origins.
   A technical sounding lane describes acoustic resource allocation. A notation
   role requires its own positive evidence from the responsible existing owner.
   Timing, staff and beam membership alone can leave musical role ambiguous.
   Missing, conflicting or stale role origin is Unknown for the affected change.
   A later migration must inventory every role-sensitive read and bind its actual
   occurrence-level requirements, including rest exemptions, rest readability,
   lower-primary continuity and primary-beam binding. It must prove preservation
   at the actual consumers or declare an independently authorized effect there.

3. The allocation owner receives current positive connection obligations from
   actual committed owners. Each obligation carries its exact occurrence
   containers, beam level, adoption epoch and complete input coverage. Adopted
   extensions retain their writer-authored identities through the same custody.
   Authenticated withdrawals advance the obligation state through their receipt.
   Historical membership alone supplies no permission to recreate a connection.

4. The human requirement to retain existing visible beams is an additional
   acceptance obligation. Evaluation binds those connections to the stable
   adopted PDF source and checks their persistence through the proposed output
   and ordinary renderer. That evaluation has no productive selection authority.
   A syntactic relation classified as incidental remains subject to this
   preservation requirement whenever it is actually visible. Any requested
   alteration needs an explicit effect scope before productive implementation.
   Source-group ancestry alone cannot waive preservation.

5. Evaluation records complete currently realized groups, incomplete original
   groups, relations crossing distinct original cohorts and Unknown evidence
   separately. Pairwise relation counts remain counts of relations at particular
   levels. They cannot stand for numbers of beams or prove that every original
   group is intact. Repairing an incomplete group needs current positive owner
   authority and an admitted repair scope.

6. Let O denote the exact represented occurrences, W their written obligations,
   R their independently justified role-sensitive requirements, Q the current
   committed beam obligations and C a proposed assignment to consumer channels.
   Let P(C) denote the real owner materialization on a temporary candidate and
   B(P(C)) its complete beam readback on the same carried identities. A candidate
   must satisfy all of the following requirements together.

   - Canonical HARD SAME, chord-host and tie requirements hold on O.
   - Each shared channel satisfies W under the accepted written-overlap and
     positive chord authority.
   - Every role-sensitive consumer satisfies R at its bound occurrences.
   - Every required connection in Q is realized in B(P(C)).
   - Each realized required group retains its complete allowed ordered input.
     Openings, closings, interruptions and attached resources are interpreted
     by the actual consumer semantics at every supported level.
   - New relations have current positive owner authority or an explicitly
     admitted effect scope.
   - Every represented carrier and its pitch spelling, written timing, staff,
     ties, rests, host and cursor obligations have complete explained custody.
   - The whole-part consumer resource model admits the complete assignment.

   Endpoint equality is one constraint within this conjunction. Complete ordered
   lane interpretation supplies an additional constraint. Cross-staff containers
   follow the actual consumer lane semantics. A staff boundary alone cannot
   decide connection membership.

7. The greedy assignment, overflow search and cross-role sharing must consume
   the same bound obligations. Partial search must preserve the possibility of
   satisfying them and complete candidates must pass the same materialization
   readback. The interacting conflict component is the unit of simultaneous
   validation. A rejected move cannot leave another required connection damaged.
   Proven infeasibility requires a concrete obstruction. An incomplete search
   reports Unknown. Omission, role promotion, force conversion and later local
   repair cannot satisfy a failed allocation obligation.

8. The implementation must originate at role derivation and allocation. Audits,
   renderer feedback, project names, measure numbers, opaque identifier values,
   corpus counts and scorewide uniqueness cannot choose a repair. Existing
   source occurrences and adopted connection identities must be transported
   through materialization. The existing authorized sink commits the accepted
   candidate and the final readback verifies that same adopted candidate.

9. Synthetic evidence must exercise the real entry points for a written endpoint
   contact with sounding overhang, independently authorized sustained voices,
   missing role origin, split-source occurrences, rests and hidden carriers,
   chord attachments, adopted and withdrawn extensions, and complete cross-staff
   lane semantics. Ordered-sequence cases include a replacing opening, an earlier
   closing, an interruption and separated endpoints while retaining every note.
   Each case also has a harmless insertion or independent-lane control.
   Renaming, relocation, valid label permutation, uniform time scaling and
   independent duplication must preserve the appropriate local decisions.
   Tests must distinguish unsupported evidence from a proved conflict.

10. Acceptance of a future musical change requires independent role/connection
    custody, synthetic causal checks, complete consumer admission, preservation
    of all declared notation and sounding obligations, and a full corpus run.
    Native final Master and Render artifacts and PDF evidence establish the
    artifact effects. The existing diagnostic canonical projection is an
    intermediate observation. Its edge counts cannot establish final visible
    preservation or grant musical authority.

This foundation contains definitions and acceptance obligations. The later
implementation must cite the accepted causal definitions unchanged, provide its
repair derivation and effect scope, and satisfy the ordinary gates. The
definitions introduce neither a role classifier nor a new connection owner.

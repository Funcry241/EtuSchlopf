# Writer-authored beam extension: evidence and transport requirements

This is a design obligation for a later implementation, not a new capability,
runtime receipt, verifier exception or authorization to write music.

## Birth and adoption

A private typed record must originate from the real connection writer's actual
input, selected operation and realized output. It must identify its authentic
source/attack/fragment occurrences, the existing group relation and precisely
the extension containers and attached chord resources it authored. Original
group membership is immutable; the extension remains a separate relation.
No reconstruction from final XML adjacency, report text, musical resemblance,
raw offsets, numeric labels or a known corpus witness may create this record.
Offsets and fingerprints serve only as locators and exact-state guards.

Evidence from a proposed, failed, discarded or superseded branch cannot become
live evidence. Only successful adoption into the corresponding Render history
can activate it. Shadow copies cannot feed their evidence back into Main.
The generic PRESERVING receipt and the observational layout journal retain their
existing semantics. A typed companion and checked custody are the intended
approach; no generic journal is silently promoted into a decision authority.

## Early binding and handover

The rest/articulation stage already receives the canonical structural plan and
sealed voice plan. Authentic source locators and producer render-occurrence
witnesses predate the bridge. The existing typed staccato persistence transport
is an implementation pattern for binding actual writer ranges to those identities.
Its existence does not prove that every bridge candidate can already be bound.
Missing, ambiguous, duplicated, wrong-source or stale bindings must not be guessed.

The FinalChord occurrence continuation is created later, after the bridge.
Therefore a bridge companion needs explicit early custody through intervening
operations, followed by a checked handover into that continuation and the final
PDF-source continuation. Moving FinalChord projection earlier is not authorized.
A future implementation must account for every actual intervening transition,
including currently unexercised branches, rather than whitelist the observed
writer sequence or rely on absence of trace rows.

## Transport and invalidation

Identity continuity and connection validity are separate obligations. A verified
enharmonic respelling can preserve identity; step/alter byte equality is not an
identity condition. Conversely, retained IDs alone cannot prove an unchanged
connection. An actual owner transition must check the participating occurrences,
timing, chord attachments, relevant lane relation and relevant beam states.
Changes may be transported only through an explicitly supported owner operation;
otherwise the extension record becomes unusable. A removed connection must not
be resurrected, and unsupported drift must never manufacture replacement evidence.
Fingerprints bind states but do not independently prove semantic equivalence.

A later lane assignment may use a live extension only under a separately defined
repair rule. It must validate the entire simultaneous batch, complete original
and extension components, attached chord members, target occupancy and conflicting
extensions. Failure may not silently authorize a partial group move. The choice
between declining an optional repair and aborting a required custody transition
must be made explicit in the implementation; neither may bypass existing gates.

## Required independent probes

Positive probes: adopted extension transported unchanged; checked enharmonic
respelling; multiple independent groups; complete chord attachment; simultaneous
safe lane move; a connection incompatible with the already available sealed plan.
Negative probes: no adoption; discarded trial; foreign source; stale pre-state;
duplicate identity; missing fragment; altered timing; lost chord attachment;
changed/removed relevant beam; conflicting destination; conflicting extension
components; and a local veto that would leave another original group separated.
Equivalent cases must survive renaming, relocation, label permutation and
independent duplication. All tests are synthetic; corpus locations are evaluation
examples only. Observability on/off must not change productive results.

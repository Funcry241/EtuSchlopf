The accepted error-class bytes remain unchanged. The current clef owner retains
all materialization, journal, receipt and adoption paths. No note content, staff
or voice projection, chord, beam, tie, slur or octave direction acquires write
permission. The existing octave-domain boundary policy and conservative fresh
beam-boundary admission remain in force, evaluated at musical time.

The supported context requires a parseable part with measured positive note
durations, checked cursor arithmetic and a checked common duration unit. Grace
or unresolved onset/time/state evidence cannot authorize a new course. Distinct
simultaneous states in incomparable cursor streams are unsupported. Ordered
surfaces in the same stream and overrides of initial declarations retain their
source-bound precedence, including equal-time first-attack materialization.
Within each voice, source-bound head order and nonoverlapping duration coverage
must be resolved before its existing structural origins can be reused.
The first clef remains fixed as the incoming declaration. Unsupported input is not labelled as a validated
baseline. This scope follows evidence and materialization constraints, not any
known score location.

The complete original-boundary and extended-boundary courses are compared with
unchanged weights under the same time relation. Expanded boundaries still need
strict whole-part improvement. A local ledger improvement alone grants no write.
Joint octave/clef reoptimization remains outside this clef-only implementation.

Independent synthetic and metamorphic evidence must cover reference events in
another stream, reordered equivalent encodings, direction offsets, mixed duration
units, ties and beams in other streams, simultaneous chords, unsupported evidence,
independent part duplication and real materialization/receipt/adoption/reentry.
Real captures are evaluation only. Native acceptance and PDF review remain
separate outcomes and are not established by this contract.

## Unsupported baseline validation

An unavailable temporal observation grants no new course. Original serialized
observations and parent postwrite tie/beam guards remain available exclusively
for validation of that unchanged unsupported baseline. Raw surface discovery is
not suppressed by unavailable time evidence. This fallback is not a temporal
proof, does not enter the corrected solver and cannot authorize an added course.
The decision entry explicitly reports unsupported context and no new course.
A synthetic negative case requires the parent guard to reject an unsafe existing
surface even when the temporal observer cannot resolve a measured time context.

The MusicXML chord onset clock and canonical structural attachment are separate
observations. Non-cursor attributes do not advance or erase the preceding note's
onset clock. The existing LowerChordObservation still owns attachment evidence;
its UNKNOWN remains UNKNOWN and cannot become a fresh boundary merely because
the musical onset is known. A synthetic case requires equal chord onset while
the original structural anchor remains unavailable and insertion remains blocked.

Overlapping or reversed visible heads can have a resolved musical clock while
voice-based structural origins remain ambiguous. Such voices supply no new
transition origins. UNKNOWN attachment or beam evidence cannot be overridden by
a fresh-boundary set. Parent-proved physical interiors remain protected when the
time-aware structural observer is unresolved; existing unsafe removable surfaces
can still be omitted by the original owner. No Source/Attack identity is inferred
from the resolved onset, voice label, pitch or document position.

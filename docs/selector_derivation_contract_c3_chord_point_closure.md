## Scope and factorization

This is a repair scope within the unchanged accepted joint-reading class, not
a new error-class definition or reader policy. The original group producer and
received material carriers remain responsible for membership and identity.
The adapter already validates complete pitched containers and an equal exact
onset for every chord group before deriving any staff-domain decision.

A carried simultaneous chord does not require all its members to use the same
clef or octave display. Those states belong to the separate physical staves.
Preserving each member's sounding spelling, time, staff and original chord
attachment preserves this point relation for every otherwise legal pair of
staff courses. There is no nonempty first-onset/last-onset interval to split.
The feasible register courses therefore remain a Cartesian product of the
existing staff domains. C3 costs and head protection stay staff-local; no new
joint-state search, cost, weight, boundary, writer or adoption rule is needed.

Only this proved point relation leaves the blanket unsupported-coupling set.
Primary beams and tied attacks spanning staves still keep every involved staff
UNKNOWN. Missing producer custody, an unbound pitched head, a non-pitched host
crossing staves, unequal onsets, foreign source epochs, unsupported capabilities
and incomplete searches retain their existing refusal or UNKNOWN behavior.
A simultaneous onset alone does not manufacture a chord group.

The shared Master/Render comparison, strict improvement requirement, original
materializers, receipts, origin registry, non-register preservation and final
artifact checks are unchanged. Removing an unsupported-input barrier does not
promise that an alternative will be writable, adopted or visually preferable.

## Evidence obligations

Synthetic probes compare the complete outcomes with the corresponding
independent domains, include hidden chord members, rename and relocate the
fixture, duplicate independent original chord observations, and exercise the
actual original producer-to-joint-input path. Negative neighbors retain unequal
onsets, resource limits and cross-staff beam/tie obligations. Existing rest-host
negative controls remain unchanged. Native acceptance and the complete fresh
XML/PDF corpus comparison remain separate required evaluation.

by measure, staff and raw voice label. If the group has no visible pitched note
and contains several rests, it retains one orientation glyph and hides the
others. Neither the raw channel nor retaining one glyph proves that the
discarded intervals are rhythmically dispensable.

retires only its final visibility writes. Its input narrows from mutable String

original receipt collection. It reveals no hidden rest, inserts no rest, changes

measure 6 and Etude 1 measure 13. The master already has hidden rest carriers;
the render subsequently replaces hidden carriers with cursor-only forwards.

corpus repair.

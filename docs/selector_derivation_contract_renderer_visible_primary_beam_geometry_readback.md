## Measurement-only classification boundary

This contract introduces no productive selector and no Beam writer. `SelectorChange: yes` records selector-sensitive audit classification under Take-Change-Control. The parent error-class definition remains byte-identical. Renderer geometry may satisfy only the already-required visibility obligation.

No known Etuede, measure or witness coordinates are encoded in the selector. Every exact cross-voice occurrence is evaluated with the same PDF-vector binding rules. Candidate multiplicity cannot change another candidate's geometry decision.

## Causal feature necessity review

1. Final renderer output is necessary because serialized MusicXML cannot prove what MuseScore visibly rendered.
2. Five-line staff-cell geometry is necessary to bind PDF vectors to a deterministic measure/staff surface without OCR or witness labels.
3. Exact stem cardinality and unique onset order are necessary because assigning PDF x-columns to MusicXML occurrences under ambiguity would create proxy identity.
4. Beam polygons spanning adjacent bound stems and geometrically intersecting both stem segments are necessary evidence of visible primary-beam continuity; raw polygon presence elsewhere in the measure or staff is insufficient.
5. Project name, measure number, pitch, voice label, page and corpus rarity are unnecessary for the causal decision and therefore cannot select class membership.
6. Renderer fragmentation remains observation only. Canonical authority and any later repair selector are independent obligations.

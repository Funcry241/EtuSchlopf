The class concerns displacement caused by a change in representation of a
source-bound sustain. A change in source demand, independently justified
reassignment of the primary course, or a genuine visibility conflict belongs
to a separate decision. A successful renderer exit supplies no evidence that
the primary event survived.

Before any productive change, a synthetic score must establish the required
sustain interval and show the displaced primary event under the old budget.
Replaying the same score with a transported independent role must conserve the
primary event and the source interval of the sustain. Adding an independent
score to the workload cannot affect eligibility. Compare the published
MusicXML and verify the existing voice, beam and temporal checks before release.

This revision records the structural class. The implementation, its
admission scope and the measurement of real-score coverage are separate.
